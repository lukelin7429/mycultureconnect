A library, not a syllabus
是圖書館，不是課綱

We do not prescribe what you teach. Every teacher has their own style and their own topics — browse, take what fits your students, leave the rest.

我們不規定你教什麼。每位老師有自己的風格與想教的主題——挑你班上用得到的，其餘留著就好。
