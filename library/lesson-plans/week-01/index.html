Opening-year intention: establish a joyful, challenging classroom culture. Use this first class as a light diagnostic—not a test. Celebrate what students already know.

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, five moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業

    Opening diagnostic: quick vocabulary, speaking, and listening challenge. No grades—just a baseline.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 20–35 min
    New learning新教學

    Diagnostic speaking + listening; sentence confidence; personal word bank.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    English Speed Round: picture naming, Chinese→English lightning round, synonym/antonym duel, and a 60-second 'All About Me' challenge.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Optional video / media plug-in影片與媒體（選用）

Optional: a short kid-friendly 'back to school' or classroom-tour clip; pause before key words and let students predict.
