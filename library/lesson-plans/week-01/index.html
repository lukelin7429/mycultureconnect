Unit 1 · English Reset & Power Vocabulary

Week 1 · Welcome Back: How Big Is Your English?

Diagnostic speaking + listening; sentence confidence; personal word bank.

Week 1 of 46 60 minutes by Dom Jones

Language target語言目標

Diagnostic speaking + listening; sentence confidence; personal word bank.

Seasonal / Taiwan connection季節與台灣連結

Opening-year intention: establish a joyful, challenging classroom culture. Use this first class as a light diagnostic—not a test. Celebrate what students already know.

Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 8 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • introducev.介紹Let me introduce myself — my name is Yi-Chen and I play basketball.introduce → introduction (n.)
  • experiencen. / v.經驗；體驗Tell me about one experience you had this summer.experience → experienced (adj.)
  • challengen. / v.挑戰Speaking English for sixty seconds is my challenge today.≈ difficult task · challenge → challenging (adj.)
  • improvev.進步；改善This year I want to improve my listening.≈ get better · ↔ get worse
  • confidentadj.有自信的She felt confident when she answered in English.↔ nervous, shy · confident → confidence (n.)
  • curiousadj.好奇的Good learners stay curious — they always ask why.↔ bored · curious → curiosity (n.)
  • favoriteadj. / n.最喜歡的What is your favorite food in your hometown?British spelling: favourite
  • discoverv.發現Today you will discover how many English words you already know.≈ find out · discover → discovery (n.)

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Opening diagnostic: quick vocabulary, speaking, and listening challenge. No grades—just a baseline.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    English Speed Round: picture naming, Chinese→English lightning round, synonym/antonym duel, and a 60-second 'All About Me' challenge.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Lightning round · Chinese → English

中翻英快問快答

For the 35–50 min speed round. Read the Chinese, students race to say the English. All eight words appear.
搭配 35–50 分鐘的 Speed Round。唸中文，學生搶答英文。八個字全部出現。

  1. 我想介紹我的家人。 → I want to introduce my family.
  2. 那是一次很棒的體驗。 → That was a great experience.
  3. 這對我來說是一個挑戰。 → This is a challenge for me.
  4. 我想改善我的發音。 → I want to improve my pronunciation.
  5. 她說英文的時候很有自信。 → She is confident when she speaks English.
  6. 我對你的暑假很好奇。 → I am curious about your summer.
  7. 你最喜歡的科目是什麼？ → What is your favorite subject?
  8. 我今天發現了一個新單字。 → I discovered a new word today.

60-second All About Me · sentence frames

六十秒自我介紹・句型架構

Put these on the board. A1 students fill in two frames; B1 and above should link them into one flowing minute.
寫在白板上。A1 學生填兩句就好；B1 以上要把句子串成流暢的一分鐘。

  1. My name is ______ and my favorite ______ is ______.
  2. One experience I will never forget is ______.
  3. This year I want to improve my ______.
  4. I feel confident when I ______.
  5. I am curious about ______.

Synonym / antonym duel

同義反義對決

Two teams. Say the word, the other team must answer with a synonym or an opposite.
分兩隊。唸出單字，另一隊要接出同義詞或反義詞。

  1. confident ↔ nervous / shy
  2. curious ↔ bored
  3. improve ↔ get worse
  4. discover ≈ find out
  5. challenge ≈ difficult task
  6. favorite ≈ the one I like best

Exit ticket · one line each

出場券・一人一句

Dom's plan asks every student to produce something before leaving. This is the smallest version that still counts.
Dom 的教案要求每個學生離開前都要產出東西。這是最小、但仍然算數的版本。

  1. Today I discovered that I already know about ______ English words.
  2. This year I want to improve my ______.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Read the lightning round off the screen. Students need one sheet of paper for their Word Bank.
不必備課。中翻英練習直接照螢幕唸。學生只需要一張紙做自己的單字銀行。

Optional video / media plug-in影片與媒體（選用）

Optional: a short kid-friendly 'back to school' or classroom-tour clip; pause before key words and let students predict.

Homework回家作業 · due next week

Build a personal English Word Bank: 10 words you already know + 5 new words. Write one sentence for 3 of the new words.
