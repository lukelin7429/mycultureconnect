The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

NOW or USUALLY · sort these

現在還是通常・分類

For Dom's Teacher Mystery. Read a sentence, students call out NOW or USUALLY, then say how they knew. The answer is always the signal word.
搭配 Dom 的 Teacher Mystery。唸出句子，學生喊 NOW 或 USUALLY，然後說出他們怎麼知道的。答案永遠是訊號字。

  1. I am holding a red pen. → NOW
  2. I drink tea every morning. → USUALLY
  3. I usually arrive at seven thirty. → USUALLY
  4. Right now you are listening to me. → NOW
  5. She is standing by the window. → ？
  6. He often forgets his homework. → ？
  7. We are currently reading a new book. → ？
  8. My father cooks dinner every day. → ？

The trap · Chinese verbs do not move

陷阱・中文的動詞不動

Chinese says 他去 whether it is he goes or he is going — the verb never changes and there is no am/is/are. So two errors arrive together this week: the missing -s, and the missing be. Show them side by side.
中文說「他去」，不管是 he goes 還是 he is going，動詞都不變，也沒有 am／is／are。所以這週會同時出現兩種錯：漏掉 -s，和漏掉 be 動詞。把它們並排給學生看。

  1. 他每天去學校。 ✗ He go to school every day. ✓ He goes to school every day.
  2. 他正在打籃球。 ✗ He playing basketball. ✓ He is playing basketball.
  3. Your turn — fix this: ✗ She study English every night.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ They watching a movie right now.

Saturday here, Saturday there

台灣的星期六，美國的星期六

Dom's culture connection. Students describe a typical Taiwanese Saturday in the present simple, then say what they are doing this particular Saturday in the continuous. Same day, two tenses.
Dom 指定的文化連結。學生先用現在簡單式描述台灣典型的星期六，再用進行式說「這個」星期六在做什麼。同一天，兩種時態。

  1. In Taiwan, students usually ______ on Saturday morning.
  2. Many families often ______ on Saturday evening.
  3. But right now, this Saturday, I am ______.
  4. In the United States, children usually ______ on Saturday. (ask the teacher!)

Exit ticket · two sentences

出場券・兩句

One habit, one action happening now. The pair is the point — one student, one moment, two tenses.
一句習慣，一句此刻。重點在成對——同一個人、同一個時刻，兩種時態。

  1. I usually ______ after school.
  2. But right now I am ______.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. The eight words split into two teams: usually / often / every day point at the present simple, right now / currently / today point at the present continuous. Teach the signal words and the tense follows.
不必備課。八個字分成兩隊：usually／often／every day 指向現在簡單式，right now／currently／today 指向現在進行式。先教訊號字，時態自己就跟上來了。
