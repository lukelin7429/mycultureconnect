  • madev.做了My grandmother made rice dumplings.不規則　make → made → made
  • tookv.拿了；搭了He took the train to Taichung.不規則　take → took → taken

Weekend Detective · the six clues

週末偵探・六條線索

Dom's activity needs clues, so here they are. Hand them out in order, one at a time. After all six, each team tells the whole story in the past tense — that telling is the real exercise.
Dom 的活動需要線索，這裡備好了。一次發一條，照順序。六條發完後，每組用過去式把整個故事講一遍——講的那一段才是真正的練習。

  1. Clue 1　On Saturday morning, Mei-Ling took the 7:40 train.
  2. Clue 2　She carried an enormous box and a small umbrella.
  3. Clue 3　At 9:00 the box was empty.
  4. Clue 4　A stranger at the station saw her laughing.
  5. Clue 5　She got home with no umbrella and a puppy.
  6. Clue 6　Her mother was not surprised.
  7. Now tell the story: What happened on Saturday? (at least 8 past-tense verbs)

The trap · Chinese marks time once

陷阱・中文只標一次時間

Chinese puts the pastness in 了 or in a time word, and the verb never moves. English moves the verb — but only once. So two opposite errors appear: no past at all, and past marked twice.
中文把過去放在「了」或時間詞上，動詞本身不動。英文是動詞要變——但只變一次。所以會出現兩種相反的錯：完全沒變，和變了兩次。

  1. 我昨天去學校。 ✗ Yesterday I go to school. ✓ Yesterday I went to school.
  2. 你昨天去了嗎？ ✗ Did you went? ✓ Did you go?　(did 已經扛了過去)
  3. Your turn — fix this: ✗ She didn't saw the message.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ Last week we visit my grandmother.

-ed has three sounds

-ed 有三種唸法

Students write -ed correctly and then say it wrong. The ending follows the sound before it — no need to memorize lists, just feel where the mouth is.
學生 -ed 寫得對，唸出來卻錯。字尾的唸法由前一個音決定——不必背清單，感覺一下嘴巴在哪裡就知道。

  1. /ɪd/ after t or d → visited · decided · wanted · needed
  2. /t/ after a voiceless sound → watched · walked · asked · finished
  3. /d/ after everything else → happened · played · listened · called
  4. Say them across: visited-watched-happened. Feel the difference?

Exit ticket · one true sentence

出場券・一句真的事

Something that really happened last weekend, with one irregular verb underlined. True beats clever.
上週末真的發生的一件事，畫出其中一個不規則動詞。真實比漂亮重要。

  1. Last weekend I ______.
  2. Underline the irregular verb you used.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Five of this week's verbs are irregular and three are regular -ed — worth sorting on the board before anything else, because that split is the whole difficulty of the past simple.
不必備課。本週五個動詞是不規則、三個是規則加 -ed——先在白板上分成兩堆，因為過去式的難處全在這條分界線上。
