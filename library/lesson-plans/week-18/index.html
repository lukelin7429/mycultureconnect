Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 9 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • ingredientn.食材What ingredients are in this sauce?可數 · 常用複數
  • appetizern.前菜We ordered two appetizers to share.美式 · 英式說 starter
  • main coursen.主菜My main course was grilled fish.兩個字 · 美式也說 entrée（注意：不是前菜）
  • dessertn.甜點Do you have room for dessert?兩個 s · desert（一個 s）是沙漠
  • spicyadj.辣的Is the beef noodle soup very spicy?hot 也可以指辣，但 hot 也指燙 · 要講清楚就用 spicy
  • sweetadj.甜的Taiwanese sausage is a little sweet.也可以形容人：she is sweet（親切）
  • savoryadj.鹹香的I prefer savory breakfasts to sweet ones.中文沒有完全對應的字 · 就是「不甜、帶鹹香」
  • vegetarianadj. / n.素食的；素食者Do you have a vegetarian option?vegan 更嚴格（不吃蛋奶）
  • allergyn.過敏I have a peanut allergy.allergy (n.) · allergic (adj.)：I'm allergic to peanuts

Restaurant Role-Play · with one surprise

餐廳角色扮演・附一個意外

Dom's role-play, with the surprise problems written out. Four roles, and the surprise goes only to the server — nobody else knows it is coming, which is what makes the English real.
Dom 的角色扮演，意外事件已經寫好。四個角色，意外只給服務生看——其他人不知道會發生什麼，英語才會是真的。

  1. Roles: customer · server · chef · food critic
  2. Surprise ① The dish the customer ordered is sold out.
  3. Surprise ② The food arrives cold.
  4. Surprise ③ There is meat in the vegetarian dish.
  5. Surprise ④ The bill is $200 more than it should be.
  6. The critic must say one kind thing and one honest thing at the end.

The trap · 'I want' is not for restaurants

陷阱・餐廳裡不說 I want

This is not grammar, it is register — and register is invisible to a learner. 我要 is neutral in Chinese; I want is blunt in English. The fix is three phrases, and they work in every shop and counter in the world.
這不是文法問題，是語域問題——而語域對學習者是看不見的。中文的「我要」是中性的，英文的 I want 卻很直。解法就三句話，全世界的店和櫃台都通用。

  1. ✗ I want a hamburger.　✓ I'll have the hamburger, please.
  2. ✓ Could I get the beef noodles?　✓ I'd like the dumplings, please.
  3. Asking: What do you recommend? · What is this dish like?
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ I want water. ✗ Give me the bill.

Your bilingual menu · six dishes

你的雙語菜單・六道菜

Dom's homework is a menu, so start it here. The hard part is not translating the name — it is describing what is in it so a foreigner can decide. One line each, ingredients plus one taste word.
Dom 的作業是菜單，在這裡先起頭。難的不是翻譯菜名，是描述裡面有什麼、讓外國人能決定要不要點。一道一句，食材加一個味道形容詞。

  1. 滷肉飯 → Braised pork rice: rice with savory slow-cooked pork. Not spicy.
  2. 鹽酥雞 → ______
  3. 珍珠奶茶 → ______
  4. 臭豆腐 → ______ (warn them!)
  5. Template: ______ : ______ with ______ . It is ______ .

Exit ticket · order something, politely

出場券・禮貌地點一道菜

One order and one question. If the order starts with 'I want', it goes back.
一句點餐、一句提問。開頭是 I want 的退回重寫。

  1. I'll have ______ , please.
  2. And could you tell me ______ ?

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. The single most useful thing you can teach this week is not a food word — it is that 'I want' does not belong in a restaurant. Chinese 我要一個漢堡 is perfectly polite; the word-for-word English sounds like a small child. Teach 'I'll have' and 'Could I get' in the first ten minutes.
不必備課。這週最有用的不是任何一個食物單字，而是「I want 不能用在餐廳」。中文說「我要一個漢堡」完全禮貌，逐字翻成英文卻像小孩子在講話。前十分鐘就把 I'll have 和 Could I get 教下去。
