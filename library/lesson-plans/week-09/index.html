  • eat · ate · eatenv.吃We have eaten all the mooncakes.-en 家族

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Third-Form Tournament: teams race to complete verb families; bonus points for correct sentences.

    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

Third-Form Tournament · the verb list

第三態錦標賽・題庫

Dom's race needs verbs, so here are twelve. Teams call out all three forms; a point for the set, a bonus point for a correct sentence using the third form.
Dom 的競賽需要題目，這裡備了十二個。各組喊出三態；答對一組得一分，能用第三態造出正確句子再加一分。

  1. speak · ______ · ______　　take · ______ · ______
  2. give · ______ · ______　　see · ______ · ______
  3. drive · ______ · ______　　forget · ______ · ______
  4. sing · ______ · ______　　drink · ______ · ______
  5. begin · ______ · ______　　swim · ______ · ______
  6. cut · ______ · ______　　put · ______ · ______
  7. (Answers: spoke/spoken · took/taken · gave/given · saw/seen · drove/driven · forgot/forgotten · sang/sung · drank/drunk · began/begun · swam/swum · cut/cut · put/put)

Three families, not thirty verbs

三個家族，不是三十個動詞

Memorizing a list of thirty is miserable. Sorting them into three shapes is a puzzle. Give students the three patterns and let them file the verbs themselves.
背三十個字很痛苦，把它們歸進三種形狀卻像解謎。給學生三種規律，讓他們自己把動詞歸檔。

  1. ① -en 家族：eat-ate-eaten · write-wrote-written · break-broke-broken · choose-chose-chosen · give-gave-given
  2. ② i–a–u 家族：sing-sang-sung · drink-drank-drunk · begin-began-begun · swim-swam-swum
  3. ③ 三態相同：cut-cut-cut · put-put-put · hit-hit-hit · let-let-let
  4. Which family does 'ring' belong to? And 'shut'?

The trap · the second form is not the third

陷阱・第二態不等於第三態

Chinese has one form per verb; English asks for three, and the second and third look similar enough to blur. After have / has / had, it must be the third. Write 'have + ③' on the board and leave it there all lesson.
中文一個動詞只有一個樣子，英文要三個，而第二態和第三態長得夠像，學生會混。have／has／had 後面一定是第三態。在白板寫下「have + ③」，整堂課都不要擦掉。

  1. ✗ I have went to Taipei. ✓ I have gone to Taipei.
  2. ✗ She has ate already. ✓ She has eaten already.
  3. Your turn — fix this: ✗ They have wrote the report.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ He has broke his glasses.

Exit ticket · start the Top 30 chart

出場券・開始做三十個動詞表

Dom's homework is a personal Top 30 chart. Start it here so nobody goes home unsure what it looks like — three columns, five rows, done in class.
Dom 的作業是做一張自己的三十動詞表。在課堂上先起頭，沒有人會回家後不知道該長什麼樣——三欄五列，當場完成。

  1. Draw three columns: base / past / past participle.
  2. Fill five verbs you will actually use this week.
  3. Write one sentence with a third form: I have ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Four of Dom's five verbs end their third form in -en — that is not a coincidence, it is a family. Point that out and fifteen forms become five patterns.
不必備課。Dom 給的五個動詞裡有四個第三態以 -en 結尾——那不是巧合，是一個家族。把這件事點破，十五個形式就變成五個規律。
