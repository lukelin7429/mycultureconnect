The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Movie Premiere: students present. Class gives positive awards: Best Vocabulary, Best Storyteller, Best English Speaker, Most Creative.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

The trap · do not apologize for your English

陷阱・不要為自己的英文道歉

In Chinese, opening with modesty is good manners. In English it is taken literally, and it tells the audience to expect something bad before a single sentence has been heard. Replace it with a real opening — the difference in how a talk lands is enormous.
中文裡謙虛開場是禮貌，英文裡會被照字面接收：一句話都還沒聽到，聽眾就先被告知「接下來會很糟」。換成真正的開場白——同一份簡報，效果差非常多。

  1. ✗ Sorry, my English is not very good…
  2. ✗ I didn't prepare very much…
  3. ✓ Good morning. Today I want to tell you about the best film I saw this year.
  4. ✓ I'm going to talk about one character who surprised me.
  5. 規則：第一句話就進入內容。道歉留給真的犯錯的時候。

Three things that change everything

三件會改變一切的事

Not language — delivery. These three are worth more than any extra vocabulary at this stage, and they can all be fixed in one rehearsal.
這三件跟語言無關，是台風。到這個階段，它們比多背單字有用得多，而且一次彩排就能修好。

  1. ① 慢下來。緊張時會加速，聽眾就跟不上。刻意比自然速度再慢一點。
  2. ② 抬頭。第一句和最後一句一定要背起來，這樣頭尾都能看著人說。
  3. ③ 停頓。講完一個重點停一秒——聽起來像有把握，不是忘詞。
  4. 彩排規則：跟同學練兩次。第一次求講完，第二次求慢。

The audience has a job too

聽眾也有任務

Without a task, the audience rehearses their own talk and hears nothing. Give every listener this card — it also guarantees that every speaker gets real feedback rather than polite silence.
沒有任務的聽眾只會在心裡排練自己的簡報，什麼都沒聽進去。發給每位聽眾這張卡——它也保證每位講者拿到的是真的回饋，不是客氣的沉默。

  1. One new word I heard: ______
  2. One thing I now want to watch: ______
  3. One question I would ask this speaker: ______
  4. One thing they did well: ______　← 這一項一定要填

The awards · everyone wins one

獎項・每個人都有一個

Dom names four; here are ten so that nobody is left out. Decide the allocation before the presentations start, and adjust as you listen. The rule is simple: every student leaves with an award and a reason.
Dom 列了四個，這裡補到十個，才不會有人落空。開始前先預排分配，邊聽邊調整。規則很簡單：每個學生都帶著一個獎和一個理由離開。

  1. Best Vocabulary · Best Storyteller · Best English Speaker · Most Creative
  2. Best Opening Line · Best Evidence · Most Convincing Recommendation
  3. Bravest First Time · Clearest Speaker · Best Question Asked
  4. Frame: The award for ______ goes to ______ , because ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Dom asks for a celebration, so protect the atmosphere: no corrections during anyone's talk, and every student gets an award. One habit to stop before the first speaker starts — the apology opening. Students say 'My English is not good' out of politeness, and an English-speaking audience hears it as a fact.
不必備課。Dom 要的是慶祝，所以守住氣氛：任何人講的時候都不糾正，而且每個學生都要拿到一個獎。第一位上台前要先止住一個習慣——道歉式開場。學生出於客氣說「我英文不好」，英語聽眾會把它當成事實照單全收。
