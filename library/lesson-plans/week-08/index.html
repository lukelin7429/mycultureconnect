Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 10 個字 · 6 個過去分詞 + 4 個訊號字 · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • visitedpp.拜訪過I have visited Tainan three times.規則　visit → visited → visited
  • eatenpp.吃過Have you ever eaten stinky tofu?不規則　eat → ate → eaten
  • seenpp.看過She has seen that movie twice.不規則　see → saw → seen
  • triedpp.試過We have tried making mooncakes at home.規則　try → tried → tried（y 變 i）
  • finishedpp.完成了I have already finished my homework.規則　finish → finished → finished
  • forgottenpp.忘記了He has forgotten his umbrella again.不規則　forget → forgot → forgotten
  • everadv.曾經Have you ever climbed Jade Mountain?只用在問句　Have you ever…?
  • neveradv.從來沒有I have never been to Japan.本身就是否定，不要再加 not
  • alreadyadv.已經We have already eaten lunch.放在 have 和分詞中間
  • yetadv.還（沒）I haven't finished it yet.用在否定句與問句 · 放句尾

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Have You Ever...? class bingo. Students ask questions and follow up with one extra question.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Have You Ever…? · class bingo

你曾經…？・全班賓果

Dom asks for bingo, so here is a grid. Students move around, ask the full question, and write a name in a square only after asking one follow-up question. The follow-up is where the real English happens.
Dom 要賓果，這裡備好九宮格。學生走動、問完整的問句，追問一題之後才能在格子裡寫名字。追問的那一句才是真正在說英文。

  1. …eaten stinky tofu?　|　…visited Taipei 101?　|　…forgotten your homework?
  2. …seen a typhoon?　|　…tried surfing?　|　…met a foreigner before?
  3. …climbed a mountain?　|　…cooked for your family?　|　…been on a night train?
  4. Follow-up must be past simple: When did you…? / How was it? / Who did you go with?

The trap · 過 and 了 go to different tenses

陷阱・「過」和「了」去了不同的時態

過 is experience with no date — that is the present perfect. 了 with a finished time is the past simple. The rule that follows: the present perfect can never sit next to a finished past time. Say 'no dates allowed' and students remember it.
「過」是沒有日期的經驗——那是現在完成式。「了」加上結束的時間點，那是過去簡單式。由此得出一條規則：現在完成式旁邊不能放結束了的時間。告訴學生「完成式不准帶日期」，他們就記得住。

  1. 我去過日本。（經驗） ✓ I have been to Japan.
  2. 我去年去了日本。（有時間） ✓ I went to Japan last year.
  3. ✗ I have gone to Japan last year.　← 完成式不准帶日期
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ I have seen that movie yesterday.
  5. Your turn — choose: She ______ (finish) her report already.

Where the four signal words sit

四個訊號字站在哪裡

Position is half the battle. ever and already go inside, between have and the participle; yet goes to the end; never replaces the not.
位置就是一半的勝負。ever 和 already 站在中間，夾在 have 和分詞之間；yet 站句尾；never 本身就是否定，不要再加 not。

  1. Have you ever eaten it?　　(ever 在中間)
  2. I have already eaten it.　　(already 在中間)
  3. I haven't eaten it yet.　　(yet 在句尾)
  4. I have never eaten it.　　(never 取代 not)
  5. ✗ I haven't never eaten it.　← 一個句子只要一個否定

Exit ticket · one experience, one date

出場券・一個經驗，一個日期

The pair proves they see the difference. Same event, two tenses, and the second one must carry a time.
成對出現才證明他們看懂了差別。同一件事，兩種時態，第二句一定要帶時間。

  1. I have ______ .　(no date allowed)
  2. I ______ last ______ .　(with a date)

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed, but read the trap card before you teach — the present perfect is the single hardest tense for Chinese speakers, and the reason is specific: 過 and 了 look alike in Chinese and land in different English tenses.
不必備課，但教之前先看一眼陷阱卡——現在完成式是中文母語者最難的一個時態，原因很具體：中文的「過」和「了」長得像，在英文裡卻落在不同的時態。
