The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Word Upgrade Auction: students 'spend' imaginary money to replace weak words with stronger, more precise choices.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Word Upgrade Auction · the lots

單字升級拍賣會・拍賣品

For the 35–50 min activity. Six weak sentences go under the hammer. Teams bid imaginary money; the best upgrade wins the lot. Pay more for precise, less for merely longer.
搭配 35–50 分鐘的活動。六個軟弱的句子上台拍賣，各組用假錢競標，升級得最好的隊伍得標。精準才值錢，只是變長不值錢。

  1. Lot 1　The night market food was good.
  2. Lot 2　Our school trip was bad.
  3. Lot 3　She was happy about her test.
  4. Lot 4　The temple is big.
  5. Lot 5　The movie was very very good.
  6. Lot 6　I had a bad day and a bad dinner.

Do not over-climb the ladder

別爬過頭

Chinese turns up the volume with 很 / 非常 / 超 on the same word, so students reach for 'very very good'. English changes the word instead — but the top rung is for big occasions. 'I was ecstatic about my sandwich' is funny, not strong. Pick the rung that fits the event.
中文用「很／非常／超」在同一個字上加大音量，所以學生會說 very very good。英文是換字——但最高一階要留給大場面。I was ecstatic about my sandwich 只會很好笑。挑對階，不是挑最高階。

  1. Your friend passed a small quiz. → happy or ecstatic?
  2. Taiwan won an Olympic gold medal. → happy or ecstatic?
  3. Your lunch box was nice today. → good or outstanding?
  4. A classmate saved someone's life. → good or outstanding?
  5. It rained during your picnic. → bad or disastrous?

Halloween word bank

萬聖節單字銀行

Dom's October connection. Same upgrade move, spooky but gentle — good for a warm-up or for the auction lots if the class wants a theme.
Dom 指定的十月連結。一樣是升級的動作，但換成萬聖節主題，嚇人而不嚇壞——可以當暖身，或直接拿來當拍賣品。

  1. scary → spooky (★★☆) → terrifying (★★★)
  2. dark → gloomy (★★☆) → pitch-black (★★★)
  3. old → ancient (★★★)
  4. strange → mysterious (★★☆) → eerie (★★★)
  5. Example: The old house was dark and scary. → The ancient house was gloomy and terrifying.

Exit ticket · upgrade and defend

出場券・升級並說明理由

Dom's homework asks students to explain two of their choices, so practise the explaining here. 'It sounds better' is not an explanation; 'it is stronger and the event was big' is.
Dom 的作業要學生說明其中兩個選擇的理由，所以在這裡先練說明。「聽起來比較好」不算理由；「這個字更強，而且那件事夠大」才算。

  1. Upgrade one word in this sentence: My weekend was good.
  2. Then finish: I chose ______ instead of ______ because ______.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Dom's four sets are ladders, not lists: each one climbs from an ordinary word to a strong one. Teach the rungs, not the words.
不必備課。Dom 這週給的四組是階梯，不是清單：每一組都從一個普通的字爬到一個有力的字。教的是階，不是字。
