The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Word Family Factory: teams transform one root word into as many useful forms as possible, then use each in a sentence.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Word Family Factory · one root, fully built

詞族工廠・示範一個字根

For the 35–50 min activity. Build this one on the board first, then hand each team a root. Ask for a sentence with every form — a form they cannot use in a sentence does not count.
搭配 35–50 分鐘的活動。先在白板上把這個字根長完，再發給每組一個字根。每種形式都要造句——造不出句子的形式不算數。

  1. care (v./n.) → I care about my grandmother.
  2. careful (adj.) → Be careful on the stairs.
  3. carefully (adv.) → He carefully opened the box.
  4. careless (adj.) → A careless mistake cost us the game.　(-less = 沒有)
  5. carelessness (n.) → Her carelessness broke the cup.
  6. Team roots: teach · help · use · friend · hope · safe

Suffix detective · the ending tells you the job

字尾偵探・字尾就是職稱

Say each word, students call out noun, adjective or adverb — from the ending alone, without knowing the meaning. That is the whole skill of this week.
唸出每個字，學生只看字尾就喊出名詞、形容詞或副詞——不必知道意思。這就是本週要練的唯一技能。

  1. kindness → ？　　darkness → ？　　(-ness = 名詞)
  2. colorful → ？　　powerful → ？　　(-ful = 形容詞)
  3. slowly → ？　　suddenly → ？　　(-ly = 副詞)
  4. invention → ？　　decision → ？　　(-tion / -sion = 名詞)
  5. unfair → ？　　unlucky → ？　　(un- = 相反)
  6. Harder — one root, all three: active · activity · actively

The trap · Chinese words do not change shape

陷阱・中文的字不會變形

In Chinese, 美 stays 美 whether it is a noun, an adjective or an adverb — the sentence decides. English changes the word itself. This is why students say 'She sings beautiful'. It is not carelessness; the first language simply has no such rule.
中文裡「美」不管當名詞、形容詞還是副詞都是「美」，靠句子決定。英文卻要把字本身改掉。這就是為什麼學生會說 She sings beautiful——不是粗心，母語裡根本沒有這條規則。

  1. 她唱得很美。 ✗ She sings beautiful. ✓ She sings beautifully.
  2. 他很有創意。 ✗ He is creation. ✓ He is creative.
  3. Your turn — fix this: ✗ The children played happy in the park.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ I felt happiness when I saw my grades.

Exit ticket · and a sign hunt

出場券・順便獵一張告示

The exit ticket first. The sign hunt is Dom's culture connection — English word families are all over Taiwanese campuses and menus once you start looking.
先寫出場券。獵告示是 Dom 指定的文化連結——開始留意之後，會發現台灣校園和菜單上到處都是英文詞族。

  1. Exit ticket: pick one root, write three forms, use one in a sentence.
  2. Sign hunt: photograph one English sign this week and bring the word family.
  3. Starters you will actually find: Recycle / Recycling / Recyclable
  4. Safety / Safe / Safely　·　Entrance / Enter　·　Delicious (menus)

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Dom sets three families rather than single words — the point is the shape of the word, not the word. The ending tells you the job: -ness / -tion / -ty make nouns, -ful / -ive make adjectives, -ly makes adverbs, un- flips the meaning.
不必備課。Dom 這週給的是三個詞族而不是三個單字——重點是字的形狀，不是字本身。字尾就是職稱：-ness／-tion／-ty 變名詞，-ful／-ive 變形容詞，-ly 變副詞，un- 把意思翻面。
