The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Create a 30-second commercial for a ridiculous product: invisible backpack, teleporting shoes, etc.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

The machinery · three parts

廣告的機器・三個零件

Take apart a real ad in five minutes with this list, then hand it back as a build kit. Students who can name the parts write far better commercials than students told to 'be persuasive'.
用這張清單五分鐘拆掉一支真廣告，再把它當成組裝說明書發回去。說得出零件名稱的學生，寫出來的廣告遠勝過只被告知「要有說服力」的學生。

  1. ① 祈使句（命令你動作）：Try it. Taste the difference. Don't wait.
  2. ② 極端形容詞：stunning · unbelievable · perfect · the only ______ you will ever need
  3. ③ 對著「你」說話：You deserve it. Your family will thank you.
  4. 加分零件：數字（9 out of 10）· 急迫（Limited time）· 問句（Tired of ______ ?）
  5. Spot check: 從一支廣告裡找出這三樣，各舉一例。

The trap · ads break the rules on purpose

陷阱・廣告是故意違規的

Students learn English from media and then copy advertising grammar into essays. Ad English is deliberately clipped and ungrammatical because it has three seconds. Name it explicitly, or a teacher will be correcting 'Think different' for months.
學生從媒體學英文，然後把廣告文法抄進作文。廣告英文故意斷句、故意不合文法，因為它只有三秒鐘。要明講，不然老師得花好幾個月改「Think different」這類句子。

  1. Think different. ← 文法上該是 differently（Apple 故意的）
  2. Got milk? ← 完整應是 Have you got milk?
  3. Because you're worth it. ← 這句沒有主要子句
  4. 規則：廣告可以這樣寫，你的作文不行。
  5. Your turn: 把上面三句改寫成完整、合文法的句子。

Ridiculous Product Pitch · the inventory

荒謬商品推銷・商品表

Dom's activity needs products, so here are eight. Draw one, get four minutes, then perform the thirty seconds. The sillier the product, the harder the language has to work — that is the point.
Dom 的活動需要商品，這裡備了八樣。抽一樣、四分鐘準備、上台講三十秒。商品越荒謬，語言就得越用力——這正是重點。

  1. an invisible backpack · teleporting shoes · homework that does itself
  2. a pillow that wakes you up gently in English · an umbrella that never blows inside out
  3. a water bottle that refills itself · glasses that translate what your teacher says
  4. a bicycle that remembers where you parked it
  5. Must include: 2 imperatives · 3 persuasive adjectives · the word 'you' at least four times
  6. Must end with: a promise and a reason to act now.

Exit ticket · one slogan

出場券・一句標語

Eight words or fewer, one imperative, one strong adjective. Short is harder than long — that is why this is the exit ticket.
八個字以內，含一個祈使句、一個強形容詞。短比長難——所以這才是出場券。

  1. Product: ______
  2. Slogan: ______

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Advertising English is built from three things students can spot in one lesson: imperatives, extreme adjectives, and the word 'you'. Once they can see the machinery, they can build it — and they also stop being fooled by it.
不必備課。廣告英文由三樣東西組成，學生一堂課就能看出來：祈使句、極端形容詞，以及 you 這個字。看懂機器怎麼運作之後，他們既能自己造一台，也比較不會被它騙。
