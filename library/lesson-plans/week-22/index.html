Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 8 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • settingn.場景；背景The setting is a night market in winter.故事的「哪裡」和「什麼時候」
  • charactern.角色The main character is a nine-year-old girl.注意：character 也指「性格」
  • conflictn.衝突The conflict starts when the money disappears.故事的引擎 · 沒有衝突就沒有故事
  • problemn.問題Her problem is that nobody believes her.比 conflict 口語 · 低年級用這個字就好
  • solutionn.解決The solution comes from the smallest character.a solution TO a problem
  • suddenlyadv.突然Suddenly, the lights went out.用來啟動衝突 · 一篇故事用一次就好
  • finallyadv.最後Finally, they found the missing envelope.收尾 · 不是 at last 的同義詞（at last 帶如釋重負）
  • meanwhileadv.同時Meanwhile, her brother was still asleep.切換到另一條線 · 第 7 週學過

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Story Dice: roll character/place/problem/action and build a class story.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Story Dice · four cups

故事骰・四個杯子

Dom's dice need faces, so here they are. Each team draws one from each cup and must use all four. The constraint is the point — a story nobody chose is easier to start than a story you have to invent from nothing.
Dom 的骰子需要骰面，這裡備好了。每組從四個杯子各抽一張，四樣都要用上。限制本身就是重點——沒得挑的故事，反而比從零想像容易開始。

  1. CHARACTER　a forgetful grandmother · a new transfer student · a stray dog · a night market vendor · a bus driver · a very serious cat
  2. PLACE　a temple at midnight · a crowded train · an empty classroom · a rooftop · a kitchen on New Year's Eve · a bus stop in the rain
  3. PROBLEM　something is missing · someone is lying · the door will not open · a promise was broken · nobody speaks the same language · the money is gone
  4. ACTION　runs away · tells the truth · hides it · asks for help · gives it back · pretends nothing happened

The trap · 'and then' eleven times

陷阱・十一個 and then

Chinese strings a story with 然後 and nobody minds. English counts repetitions, and 'and then' five times in a row is the clearest sign of a beginner. Give them six replacements and ban the phrase for one draft.
中文用「然後」串故事，沒有人會覺得奇怪。英文會數重複，連續五個 and then 是最明顯的初學者標記。給他們六個替代品，然後規定這一稿禁用。

  1. 然後 → after that · a moment later · the next morning · without thinking · while she waited · by the time he arrived
  2. ✗ She opened the door and then she saw the box and then she opened it.
  3. ✓ She opened the door. Inside was a box. Without thinking, she opened it.
  4. Rule for this draft: you may use 'and then' exactly once.

The shape of a story · five beats

故事的形狀・五個拍子

Draw this as a line that climbs and drops. Dom's homework is a 150-word outline, so fill the five beats in class and the outline is done — one sentence per beat is enough.
把它畫成一條上升再落下的線。Dom 的作業是 150 字大綱，課堂上把五個拍子填完，大綱就完成了——一個拍子一句話就夠。

  1. ① Setting　Where and when? One sentence.
  2. ② Character　Who wants something? What do they want?
  3. ③ Problem　What gets in the way?
  4. ④ Worst moment　When does it look hopeless?
  5. ⑤ Ending　What changes? (Do not write a moral — let the story say it.)

Exit ticket · one sentence that starts a story

出場券・一句可以開頭的話

The first line only. It must make someone want the second line — a name and a problem in the same sentence usually does it.
只寫第一句。這句要讓人想看第二句——通常一個名字加一個問題放在同一句就成了。

  1. First line: ______

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. The story dice are written out below — copy the four lists onto the board, or cut them up and put them in four cups. The one thing to fix this week is 'and then', which will otherwise appear eleven times in every story.
不必備課。故事骰的內容已經寫在下面——把四張清單抄上白板，或剪開放進四個杯子。這週唯一要修的是 and then，否則每篇故事都會出現十一次。
