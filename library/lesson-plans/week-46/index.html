The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Final game-show: vocabulary ladder, irregular verbs, mystery picture, synonym/antonym duel, grammar lightning round, and class superlatives.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

The Great English Challenge · six rounds

英語大挑戰・六個回合

Dom names six rounds; here is what to actually say in each. Two teams, a scoreboard on the board, and no round longer than seven minutes. Speed is what makes it feel like a celebration rather than a test.
Dom 列了六個回合，這裡寫出每個回合實際要說什麼。兩隊、白板上記分、每回合不超過七分鐘。節奏快，它才像慶祝而不是考試。

  1. ① 單字階梯（W4、W13）：good → ? → ? 　· bad → ? 　· happy → ? → ? 　· big → ? → ?
  2. ② 不規則動詞（W9）：go / eat / write / break / choose / drink / begin / put — 搶答三態
  3. ③ 神秘圖片（W24）：描述一張照片但不能說出它是什麼，對手猜。必須用到 There is/are。
  4. ④ 同義反義對決（W11、W12）：furious / ancient / generous / spacious / exhausted
  5. ⑤ 文法快問（W5–W33）：老師唸一句錯的，搶答改正。✗ He go every day. ✗ If it will rain… ✗ I have went…
  6. ⑥ 班級之最：Most Improved · Most Likely to Teach English One Day · Best Question of the Year

Look how far · ask Week 1 again

回頭看・把第 1 週的問題再問一次

In Week 1 this class was asked how big their English was, with no grades and no pressure. Ask the same question now and put the two answers side by side. For most students this is the first time they have seen their own progress as a number.
第 1 週問過這個班「你的英文有多大」，不打分數、沒有壓力。現在再問一次，把兩個答案並排。對多數學生來說，這是他們第一次看見自己的進步變成一個具體的數字。

  1. In September I thought I knew about ______ English words.
  2. Now I think I know about ______ .
  3. Five things I can do in English now that were harder in September:
  4. ______ · ______ · ______ · ______ · ______
  5. The hardest thing I understand now: ______

Summer English Mission

暑假英語任務

Dom's summer bridge. One thing a week, twenty words, nothing else. A small mission that gets done beats a big one that does not — and it keeps the year from leaking away over the break.
Dom 設計的暑假銜接。一週一件事、二十個字，不多。做得完的小任務勝過做不完的大任務——也讓這一年不會在暑假漏光。

  1. 每週選一樣：一首英文歌 · 一支影片 · 一部電影 · 一集影集
  2. 每週記下：二十個字（不必是新字，是你想留下的字）
  3. 每週寫一句：This week I watched ______ and my favourite line was " ______ ."
  4. 開學帶回來：你的 Summer English Journal 和一個推薦。
  5. Rule: 漏掉一週沒關係，不要因此整個放棄。

Exit ticket · the last one of the year

出場券・今年最後一張

Forty-six weeks of exit tickets end here. This one is not marked — it is for the student to keep, and it is worth telling them that.
四十六週的出場券到這裡結束。這一張不評分——是留給學生自己的，而且值得告訴他們這件事。

  1. The most useful thing I learned this year: ______
  2. One sentence I could not say in September, and can say now:
  3. " ______ "
  4. Next year I want to ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed — the whole game show is written out below, six rounds ready to run. This is the last page of the year, so keep one thing in reserve: Week 1 asked students to guess how many English words they knew. Ask again at the end. That comparison is the real closing ceremony.
不必備課——整場遊戲節目已經寫在下面，六個回合可以直接跑。這是一年的最後一頁，所以留一手：第 1 週問過學生「你覺得你會幾個英文字」。最後再問一次。那個對照才是真正的結業式。
