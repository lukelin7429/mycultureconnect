Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 8 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • was / werev.（過去的 be 動詞）I was reading when the phone rang.I/he/she/it → was　you/we/they → were
  • whileconj.當…的時候（長動作）While I was cooking, my brother was singing.while 後面通常接 was/were + -ing
  • whenconj.當…的時候（短動作）I was walking home when it started to rain.when 後面通常接過去簡單式
  • suddenlyadv.突然Suddenly, the lights went out.放句首製造戲劇效果
  • meanwhileadv.同時Meanwhile, her sister was still sleeping.連接兩個同時進行的場景
  • happenv.發生What happened while I was away?happen → happened（規則）
  • interruptv.打斷A loud knock interrupted our meeting.interrupt → interruption (n.)
  • noticev.注意到Nobody noticed that the door was open.notice → noticed（規則）

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Freeze Frame: show a picture and ask, 'What was happening when...?' Students create unexpected interruptions.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Freeze Frame · five backgrounds

定格・五個背景

For Dom's activity. Read the background, students supply the interruption. The background stays in was/were + -ing; the interruption jumps to past simple. Worked example first.
搭配 Dom 的活動。老師唸背景，學生補上打斷。背景維持 was/were + -ing，打斷跳到過去簡單式。先看示範。

  1. Example: I was eating my lunch when a monkey took my banana.
  2. We were practicing for the concert when ______.
  3. While the teacher was writing on the board, ______.
  4. My father was driving home when ______.
  5. The whole class was taking a test when ______.
  6. While we were waiting for the bus, ______.

The trap · one Chinese word, two English ones

陷阱・中文一個詞，英文兩個

Chinese uses 的時候 for both. English splits it: while for the long action, when for the short one. If both actions are long, while works for both. If both are short, use when.
中文兩種情況都用「的時候」。英文分家了：while 給長動作，when 給短動作。兩個都長，兩個都用 while；兩個都短，用 when。

  1. 我在洗澡的時候電話響了。 ✓ I was taking a shower when the phone rang.　(長 → when 接短)
  2. 我在洗澡的時候我弟在唱歌。 ✓ While I was taking a shower, my brother was singing.　(兩個都長)
  3. Your turn — fix this: ✗ While the phone rang, I was cooking.
  4. Your turn — choose: I ______ (walk) to school when I ______ (see) my teacher.

Halloween interruptions

萬聖節的打斷

Dom's Halloween week. Spooky but gentle — the sillier the interruption, the better the grammar sticks.
Dom 指定的萬聖節週。嚇人但別嚇壞——打斷越荒謬，文法記得越牢。

  1. I was walking past the old temple when ______.
  2. While my sister was watching a scary movie, ______.
  3. We were handing out candy when suddenly ______.

Exit ticket · one interrupted moment

出場券・一個被打斷的瞬間

One sentence, both tenses, and it has to be true.
一句話，兩種時態，而且要是真的。

  1. I was ______ when ______.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. The whole week hangs on one contrast: the long action in the background (was doing) and the short action that cuts across it (did). Draw a long line on the board and a short arrow through it — that drawing is the grammar.
不必備課。整週只靠一組對比：背景那個長動作（was doing），和橫切過去的短動作（did）。在白板上畫一條長線，再畫一支箭穿過去——那張圖就是文法。
