The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Time Machine game: students jump to 'yesterday,' 'right now,' 'in my life,' or 'when I was younger' and speak accordingly.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Which tense? · three questions

用哪個時態？・三個問題

Not a rule to memorize — a path to walk. Ask the three questions in order and the tense falls out. Put this on the board and leave it up for the rest of the year.
這不是要背的規則，是一條可以走的路。照順序問三個問題，時態自己就掉出來了。把它寫在白板上，接下來一整年都留著。

  1. ① Is it finished? No → is it happening right now? Yes → am/is/are + -ing. No → present simple.
  2. ② Is it finished? Yes → do you say when? Yes → past simple.
  3. ③ Finished but no date, or still true today? → have/has + third form.
  4. Test it: 我學英文六年了。→ still true today → I have studied English for six years.

Time Machine · four zones

時光機・四個區

Dom's game. Mark four corners of the room. Call a zone, students move and must speak in the matching tense within five seconds. Anyone who uses the wrong tense goes back to the middle.
Dom 的遊戲。在教室四角標出四個區。喊一個區，學生移動過去，五秒內用對應時態說一句話。用錯時態的回到中間。

  1. Corner 1　YESTERDAY → past simple　(I ate breakfast at seven.)
  2. Corner 2　RIGHT NOW → present continuous　(I am standing in the corner.)
  3. Corner 3　IN MY LIFE → present perfect　(I have visited Kenting twice.)
  4. Corner 4　WHEN I WAS YOUNGER → past simple / used to　(I played every afternoon.)

since or for?

since 還是 for？

Both become 了 in Chinese, so students guess. since takes a point on the calendar; for takes a length. If you can answer 'when did it start?', use since. If you can answer 'how long?', use for.
兩個在中文裡都變成「了」，所以學生只能猜。since 接日曆上的一點，for 接一段長度。能回答「什麼時候開始的」用 since，能回答「多久」用 for。

  1. ______ 2020　·　______ six years　·　______ I was eight　·　______ a long time
  2. ______ last summer　·　______ three months　·　______ Monday　·　______ ten minutes
  3. Your turn: 我在這間學校待了三年。→ I have been at this school ______ three years.
  4. Your turn: 我從三年級就住在彰化。→ I have lived in Changhua ______ third grade.

Exit ticket · the 2026 time capsule

出場券・2026 時光膠囊

Dom's seasonal reset. Four sentences, four tenses, sealed and opened at the end of the year. This doubles as the start of the homework timeline.
Dom 指定的季節重整。四句話、四種時態，封起來、年底再打開。這也正好是回家作業那張時間軸的開頭。

  1. I usually ______ .　(現在簡單式)
  2. Right now I am ______ .　(現在進行式)
  3. Last month I ______ .　(過去簡單式)
  4. So far this year I have ______ .　(現在完成式)

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. This is the review week, so the nine words are all time signals — each one points at a tense. Teach the signals and the review teaches itself.
不必備課。這是複習週，所以九個字全是時間訊號——每一個都指向一種時態。把訊號教好，複習就自己完成了。
