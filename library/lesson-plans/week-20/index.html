60 minutes, four moves

The politeness ladder

禮貌階梯

Same request, five heights. Read them down the ladder and students hear the change immediately — no explanation needed. Then give them a situation and ask which rung it deserves.
同一個要求，五種高度。從上往下唸一遍，學生立刻聽得出差別，不必解釋。然後給一個情境，問他們該用哪一階。

  1. ① Give me a refund.　　　　← 命令，幾乎沒有人這樣說
  2. ② I want a refund.　　　　← 很直
  3. ③ Can I get a refund?　　　← 一般
  4. ④ Could I get a refund, please?　← 客氣
  5. ⑤ I was wondering if I could get a refund.　← 很客氣，用在對方可能會拒絕的時候
  6. Which rung: 便利商店找錯零錢？飯店把你的訂房弄丟了？

The trap · 'please' does not rescue a blunt sentence

陷阱・加 please 救不了一句直白的話

Students learn that 請 makes things polite and assume please does the same job. It does not — please softens an already-polite structure. Rebuild the sentence first, then add please if you like.
學生學到「請」能讓話變客氣，就以為 please 也一樣。不是的——please 是替「本來就客氣的句型」加分。先把句子重蓋，之後要不要加 please 隨你。

  1. ✗ Please give me a refund.　→　✓ Could I get a refund, please?
  2. ✗ Please repeat.　→　✓ Sorry, could you say that again?
  3. ✗ Please help me.　→　✓ Excuse me, could you help me for a second?
  4. Your turn — rebuild: ✗ Please change my room.

Customer Service Challenge · four cases

客服大挑戰・四個案件

Dom's challenge, written as cases. Pairs take one each: one is the customer, one is the staff. The staff member must apologize, offer a solution, and the customer must accept or push politely. Swap roles and run again.
Dom 的挑戰，寫成案件。兩人一組各拿一件：一個當客人、一個當店員。店員要道歉並提出解法，客人要客氣地接受或再爭取。角色互換再跑一次。

  1. Case 1　Your luggage did not arrive in New York.
  2. Case 2　The phone you bought last week is already broken.
  3. Case 3　The delivery came three days late and one item is missing.
  4. Case 4　You ordered vegetarian noodles and there is pork in them.
  5. Staff must use: I'm so sorry about that. · Let me see what I can do. · Could I offer you ______ ?

Exit ticket · climb one rung

出場券・往上爬一階

Take a blunt sentence and rebuild it twice — once polite, once very polite.
拿一句直白的話，重蓋兩次——一次客氣，一次非常客氣。

  1. Blunt: Change my seat.
  2. Polite: ______
  3. Very polite: ______

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed, but this is the week to say the thing out loud that explains the whole unit: in English, politeness is built with grammar, not with tone. The longer and more indirect the sentence, the more polite it sounds. Chinese does it with 請 and with how you say it. That difference is why a fluent student can still sound rude.
不必備課，但這是該把整個單元的道理講明的一週：英文的禮貌是用文法蓋出來的，不是用語氣。句子越長、越間接，聽起來越客氣。中文靠「請」和說話的方式。這個差別，正是為什麼一個講得很流利的學生還是可能聽起來很兇。
