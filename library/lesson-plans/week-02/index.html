Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 8 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • quietadj.安靜的The library was quiet, so we could hear the rain outside.↔ noisy, loud · quiet (adj.) → quietly (adv.)
  • enormousadj.巨大的My grandmother brought out an enormous mooncake after dinner.≈ huge, gigantic · ↔ tiny
  • quicklyadv.很快地He quickly wrote the new word in his notebook.↔ slowly · quick (adj.) → quickly (adv.)
  • carefullyadv.小心地；仔細地She carefully carried the hot soup to the table.↔ carelessly · careful (adj.) → carefully (adv.)
  • excitingadj.令人興奮的The last ten minutes of the game were exciting.↔ boring · 小心：exciting 是「令人興奮的」，excited 是「（人）感到興奮的」
  • surprisinglyadv.出乎意料地Surprisingly, my little brother finished the race first.surprise (n./v.) → surprising (adj.) → surprisingly (adv.)
  • althoughconj.雖然Although it rained, we still went outside to look at the moon.≈ though, even though · 小心：英文只用一個連接詞，不說 Although…, but…
  • becauseconj.因為We had a barbecue because it was Mid-Autumn Festival.because 接原因，so 接結果 · 小心：不能同一句用「Because…, so…」

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    The Sentence Builder: start with 'I like dogs.' and keep adding useful information until the class creates a mini-story.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

The Sentence Builder · a worked ladder

句子養成梯・示範

For the 35–50 min activity. Show this ladder first so students see where they are going, then start a new one as a class. Every rung adds one kind of information.
搭配 35–50 分鐘的活動。先示範這道梯子，讓學生看見終點在哪，再全班一起爬一道新的。每一階只加一種訊息。

  1. I like dogs.
  2. + adjective → I like enormous dogs.
  3. + adverb → I quietly play with enormous dogs.
  4. + because → I quietly play with enormous dogs because they are friendly.
  5. + although → Although my mother says no, I quietly play with the enormous dog next door because he is surprisingly friendly. (19 words)
  6. Now start a new ladder: “We eat mooncakes.” / “The class is noisy.” / “She runs.”

Upgrade drill · 5 words → 12 words

句子升級・五個字變十二個字

Two traps from Chinese

兩個中文帶來的陷阱

Both of this week's conjunctions have a matching pair in Chinese that English does not use. Expect these errors — they are not carelessness, they are the first language showing through. Fix them together.
本週兩個連接詞在中文裡都是成對出現，英文卻只用一個。學生一定會犯——那不是粗心，是母語在說話。一起把它們改過來。

  1. 雖然下雨，但是我們還是出門了。 ✗ Although it rained, but we still went out. ✓ Although it rained, we still went out.
  2. 因為月亮很亮，所以我們在外面吃。 ✗ Because the moon was bright, so we ate outside. ✓ Because the moon was bright, we ate outside.
  3. Your turn — fix this: ✗ Although the mooncake was enormous, but I finished it.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ Because I practiced carefully, so I improved quickly.

Exit ticket · one long sentence

出場券・一個長句

One sentence about Mid-Autumn Festival, at least twelve words, using because or although and one word from this week.
寫一句中秋節的句子，至少十二個字，要用到 because 或 although，加上本週任一個單字。

  1. Example: Although the night was quiet, our family laughed a lot because we barbecued together.
  2. Your sentence: ______

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Write 'I like dogs.' on the board and build from there. Note that this week's eight words are the lesson itself: three adjectives, three adverbs, two conjunctions — the exact materials a sentence grows with.
不必備課。在白板寫下「I like dogs.」就能開始。請注意：本週八個字本身就是這堂課——三個形容詞、三個副詞、兩個連接詞，正好是把句子養大的三種材料。
