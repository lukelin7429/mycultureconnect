Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 摘要工具 · 8 個句型 · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • The film is set in…phr.這部片的背景是…The film is set in a small town in the 1980s.開場第一句 · 現在式
  • The story follows…phr.故事講的是…The story follows a boy who loses his dog.接主角 · 比 It is about 好
  • At the beginning…phr.一開始…At the beginning, nobody believes him.時間標記，不是時態標記
  • Everything changes when…phr.轉折發生在…Everything changes when she finds the letter.衝突進場的那一句
  • Meanwhile…adv.與此同時…Meanwhile, his sister is searching the other side of town.第 7、22 週學過
  • As a result…phr.結果…As a result, the two families finally meet.因果 · 比 so 正式
  • In the end…phr.最後…In the end, he keeps the dog.✗ At the end（那是指「在結尾處」）
  • What makes it memorable is…phr.讓人難忘的是…What makes it memorable is the silence in the last scene.收尾句 · 從摘要轉向意見

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Movie Mission: students track setting, character, problem, important vocabulary, and one surprising moment.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

The trap · film summaries live in the present

陷阱・電影摘要活在現在式

English writes about what happens inside a story in the present tense, even for a film made forty years ago. Chinese makes no such distinction, and neither does common sense, so students write the whole summary in the past. Say the rule once, then hold them to it.
英文描述「故事裡發生的事」用現在式，就算那部片是四十年前拍的。中文沒有這個區分，直覺上也想不到，所以學生會整篇寫成過去式。把規則講一次，然後堅持執行。

  1. ✗ A boy lost his dog and searched the whole city.
  2. ✓ A boy loses his dog and searches the whole city.
  3. ✓ The film was made in 1988.　← 講「這部片」本身，才用過去式
  4. 區分：故事裡面 → 現在式　·　拍片這件事 → 過去式
  5. Your turn — fix this: ✗ In the end, she found her father and they went home.

Summary in 150 words · the shape

150 字摘要・骨架

Dom's homework is a plot summary. Give them the shape or they will retell the entire film. Five sentences, present tense, no ending spoiler unless it is needed to make sense.
Dom 的作業是劇情摘要。不給骨架，他們會把整部片重講一遍。五句話、現在式，除非不講結局會看不懂，否則不要爆雷。

  1. ① The film is set in ______ and follows ______ .
  2. ② At the beginning, ______ .
  3. ③ Everything changes when ______ .
  4. ④ As a result, ______ .
  5. ⑤ In the end, ______ .
  6. Rule: 五句寫完就停。寫超過的人，通常是在重講不是在摘要。

Exit ticket · one sentence, present tense

出場券・一句話，現在式

The opening line of their summary, written before they leave. Past tense sends it back.
摘要的第一句，離開前寫完。寫成過去式的退回。

  1. The film is set in ______ and follows ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. The vocabulary this week is the student's own, so what follows is the toolkit for writing the summary Dom sets. One rule decides the whole homework: film summaries are written in the present tense, and almost every student will write it in the past.
不必備課。這週的字彙由學生自己選，所以下面給的是寫 Dom 那份摘要的工具。有一條規則決定整份作業的成敗：電影摘要用現在式寫，而幾乎每個學生都會寫成過去式。
