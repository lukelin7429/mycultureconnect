Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 9 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • pricen.價格The price includes tax.可數 · What's the price? = How much is it?
  • discountn.折扣Students get a ten percent discount.注意：中文「打八折」= 20% off，不是 80% off
  • receiptn.收據；發票Keep your receipt if you want to return it.p 不發音：/rɪˈsiːt/
  • sizen.尺寸Do you have this in a bigger size?What size are you? / in a different size
  • changen.零錢；找的錢Here is your change.這個意思不可數 · ✗ two changes
  • expensiveadj.昂貴的Night market food is not expensive.形容「東西」· ✗ The price is expensive ✓ The price is high
  • affordableadj.負擔得起的We found an affordable hotel near the station.afford (v.) → affordable · I can't afford it
  • quantityn.數量Buy a small quantity first.quantity 數量 · quality 品質 · 兩個字很像，別混
  • enoughadj. / adv.足夠的I don't have enough money.放名詞「前面」，但放形容詞「後面」：enough money / good enough

Class Store · the shopping list

教室商店・採購清單

Dom's store needs stock and a silly list. Give each team 500 imaginary dollars and this list; they must buy, ask prices, request a discount and check their change — all in English. Running out of money is part of the fun.
Dom 的商店需要商品和一張荒謬的清單。給每組五百元假錢和這張清單，要用英語買東西、問價錢、殺價、確認找零。錢不夠用正是樂趣所在。

  1. Shop stock: bubble tea $50 · notebook $35 · umbrella $120 · sneakers $890 · rice ball $25 · headphones $600
  2. Your list: something to drink · something to write with · something for rain · a gift under $100
  3. You must ask: How much is…? · Do you have it in another size? · Is there a discount for students?
  4. Before you leave: Can I have a receipt? · Is this the right change?

The trap · much, many, and the missing -s

陷阱・much、many，和消失的 -s

Chinese counts with measure words and never adds -s, so two errors arrive together: the plural goes missing, and much/many gets picked at random. The test is simple — can you put a number directly in front of it?
中文用量詞數數，而且永遠不加 -s，所以兩種錯會一起出現：複數不見了，much／many 也是亂猜的。判準很簡單——這個字前面能不能直接放一個數字？

  1. 能放數字 → 可數 → many / a few：three books · many students
  2. 不能放數字 → 不可數 → much / a little：✗ three moneys ✓ much money · ✗ two luggages ✓ much luggage
  3. ✗ How many money do you have? ✓ How much money do you have?
  4. Your turn — choose: I don't have ______ (much / many) time.
  5. Your turn — fix this: ✗ There were too much people in the store.

enough sits on both sides

enough 站在兩邊

One word, two positions, and Chinese gives no clue which. Before a noun, after an adjective. Say the pair aloud ten times and it sticks.
同一個字，兩種位置，中文完全給不了提示。名詞在後、形容詞在前。把這一對唸個十次就記住了。

  1. 名詞 → enough money · enough time · enough seats
  2. 形容詞 → good enough · big enough · cheap enough
  3. ✗ money enough　✗ enough good
  4. Your turn: This shirt is not ______ ______ for me. (big / enough)

Exit ticket · one thing you cannot afford

出場券・一件你買不起的東西

Two sentences, one countable noun and one uncountable, used correctly.
兩句話，一個可數名詞、一個不可數名詞，都要用對。

  1. I would like to buy ______ , but I don't have enough ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Dom puts count and non-count nouns in this week for a reason: Chinese has no plural -s and counts everything with a measure word (一張、一杯、一個). English splits nouns into two kinds and changes the question word. This is the week to fix 'How many money?' for good.
不必備課。Dom 把可數與不可數放進這週是有道理的：中文沒有複數 -s，什麼都用量詞數（一張、一杯、一個）。英文把名詞分成兩種，連問句的疑問詞都跟著換。這是把 How many money 徹底改掉的一週。
