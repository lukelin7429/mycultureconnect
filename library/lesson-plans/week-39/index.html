The trap · there is no one correct English

陷阱・沒有唯一正確的英語

Students think American English is the real one and everything else is a deviation — so they freeze when they meet an Indian or Singaporean speaker, and they are ashamed of their own accent. More people speak English as a second language than as a first. Say that number out loud.
學生以為美式英語才是真的，其他都是偏離——所以遇到印度或新加坡口音就當機，也為自己的口音感到羞恥。全世界把英語當第二語言的人，比母語者還多。把這個事實講出來。

  1. 說英語的人裡，非母語者多於母語者。
  2. 沒有人「沒有口音」——美國人也有口音，只是你聽習慣了。
  3. 目標不是聽起來像美國人，是「被聽懂」。
  4. 台灣人講的英語也是一種英語，它讓台灣被世界聽見。
  5. Ask the class: 你聽過幾種英語口音？在哪裡聽到的？

Accent Detective · same meaning, different word

口音偵探・同義不同字

Dom's activity, with the map started. Her homework asks for ten pairs, so fill these and let teams hunt for the rest. The point is not to memorize which is which — it is to stop panicking when the unfamiliar one appears.
Dom 的活動，地圖先起個頭。她的作業要十組，這裡填完，其餘讓各組自己找。重點不是背哪個是哪國，而是遇到不熟的那個時不要慌。

  1. elevator / lift　·　apartment / flat　·　vacation / holiday
  2. fries / chips　·　cookie / biscuit　·　soccer / football
  3. trash / rubbish　·　subway / underground　·　line / queue
  4. fall / autumn　·　truck / lorry　·　pants / trousers　← 這組誤會最大
  5. Bonus: 台灣的英語裡有哪些字是我們自己的？（例如 scooter、night market）

Listening across accents

跨口音聽力

Play three short clips from three places without saying where they are from. Students write what they understood, then guess the country. The surprise is usually that they understood more than they expected.
播三段來自不同地方的短片，先不說是哪裡。學生寫下聽懂的內容，再猜國家。通常的驚喜是：他們聽懂的比自己以為的多。

  1. Clip 1 ______ 　聽懂多少？ ______ %
  2. Clip 2 ______ 　聽懂多少？ ______ %
  3. Clip 3 ______ 　聽懂多少？ ______ %
  4. Which was easiest? Why — the speed, the words, or just familiarity?
  5. Rule: 「聽不懂」要說成「我還不習慣這個口音」——那是實話，也比較準確。

Exit ticket · your English, your place

出場券・你的英語，你的位置

Dom asks for Taiwan's place in the English-speaking world, so end on it. One sentence, and it should be about being understood, not about sounding like someone else.
Dom 要求談台灣在英語世界的位置，就收在這裡。一句話，重點放在「被聽懂」，不是「像誰」。

  1. I use English to ______ .
  2. One day I want to explain ______ to someone from another country.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Taiwan teaches American English, so students assume everything else is wrong — including their own. Dom's note asks you to celebrate English as an international language and to include Taiwan in it. That framing is the lesson: being understood matters more than sounding American.
不必備課。台灣教美式英語，所以學生會以為其他都是錯的——包括自己的。Dom 在教案裡要求把英語當成國際語言來慶祝，而且要把台灣放進去。這個定位就是這堂課：被聽懂，比聽起來像美國人重要。
