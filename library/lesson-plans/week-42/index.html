The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Vocabulary Detective Lab: students infer meanings from scenes, then verify and build word families/synonyms.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Guess before you look

先猜再查

Four kinds of clue, and students should try all four before opening anything. A guessed word sticks; a looked-up word evaporates by Friday.
四種線索，四種都試過再查。猜出來的字會留下，查出來的字禮拜五就蒸發了。

  1. ① 句子裡的線索：He was so exhausted he fell asleep at the table. → exhausted ≈ ?
  2. ② 字的零件：un- / -ful / -tion（第 3、12 週）→ unbelievable 拆開看看
  3. ③ 畫面與語氣：角色的表情、聲音，已經告訴你一半
  4. ④ 前後句：下一句常常就在解釋上一句
  5. 規則：四種都試過，才可以查。

Which words are worth keeping?

哪些字值得留下來？

Dom asks students to choose words they genuinely want to use. Give them a test, or they will collect the rarest and most useless words in the film because those felt hardest.
Dom 要學生挑「真的想用」的字。給他們一個判準，否則他們會收集全片最罕見最沒用的字——因為那些感覺最難。

  1. 問自己：我下個月還會再遇到這個字嗎？
  2. 值得留：exhausted · figure out · disappointed · promise · deserve
  3. 不必留：swashbuckling · thou · newfangled（一輩子遇到三次）
  4. 特別值得留：片語動詞（figure out, hang out）——課本教得少，真實英文裡到處都是
  5. Your turn: 從你的片單挑三個留、三個丟，各說一句理由。

Vocabulary poster · one word, five boxes

單字海報・一個字，五個格子

Dom's homework is a twelve-word poster. Do one together here so the format is clear. The sentence box is the only one that proves anything — insist it is about the student's own life, not the film.
Dom 的作業是十二個字的海報。這裡一起做一個，格式就清楚了。只有造句那一格能證明學會了——規定句子要寫自己的生活，不是電影劇情。

  1. WORD: exhausted
  2. Definition (自己的話): very, very tired — too tired to continue
  3. 中文: 精疲力竭的
  4. Synonym / Antonym: worn out / energetic　(極端字，不加 very — 第 13 週)
  5. My own sentence: After the sports day relay, I was completely exhausted.
  6. Now do one of your own.

Exit ticket · one word you guessed right

出場券・一個你猜對的字

One word they worked out from context before checking, plus the clue that gave it away. The clue is the skill.
寫一個他們先從上下文猜出、之後才確認的字，再寫出是哪個線索洩漏了答案。線索才是技能。

  1. Word: ______
  2. I guessed it because ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. The words are the students' own this week, so the skill is choosing and inferring rather than memorizing. Two habits to break: reaching for the dictionary at the first unknown word, and keeping words they will never meet again.
不必備課。這週的字由學生自己挑，所以要練的是「怎麼挑、怎麼猜」，不是背。有兩個習慣要改：遇到第一個生字就查字典，以及留下一堆這輩子不會再遇到的字。
