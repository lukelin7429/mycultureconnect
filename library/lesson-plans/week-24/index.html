The trap · 有 is not 'have'

陷阱・「有」不是 have

This is the single most recognizable Taiwanese English error, and it comes straight from 有. A place does not 'have' things; there ARE things in it. Once a student hears the difference they rarely go back.
這是最好認的台灣英語錯誤，直接來自「有」。地方不是 have 東西，而是「那裡有」= there is / there are。學生一旦聽出差別，很少再錯回去。

  1. 夜市有很多人。 ✗ The night market has many people. ✓ There are many people in the night market.
  2. 我房間有一張書桌。 ✗ My room has a desk. ✓ There is a desk in my room.
  3. （have 用在「人擁有」）✓ I have a desk. ✓ She has two brothers.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ Our school has a big library and has many trees.

in, on, at · where Chinese uses one word

in、on、at・中文只用一個「在」

Three English words, one Chinese word, and no logic that transfers. Teach it as sizes: at is a point, on is a surface or a line, in is inside something. The exceptions are learned by ear, not by rule.
英文三個字、中文一個「在」，而且沒有可以搬過來的邏輯。當成大小來教：at 是一個點，on 是面或線，in 是裡面。例外靠耳朵記，不靠規則。

  1. at → at the bus stop · at school · at the corner　(一個點)
  2. on → on the table · on the third floor · on Zhongshan Road　(面或線)
  3. in → in the classroom · in Changhua · in my bag　(裡面)
  4. 注意：in the bus 還是 on the bus？→ on the bus（交通工具用 on）
  5. Your turn: The night market is ______ Minzu Road, ______ Beidou.

Mystery Place · with all five senses

神秘地點・五感俱全

Dom's guessing game, and her homework asks for all five senses. Most students write only what they see, so require one line per sense before anyone is allowed to guess.
Dom 的猜謎遊戲，她的作業要求五感俱全。多數學生只寫看到的，所以規定每一種感官都要寫一句，才能開放猜。

  1. See: ______
  2. Hear: ______
  3. Smell: ______ ← 這一句通常最有辨識度
  4. Taste or touch: ______
  5. Feel (the mood): ______
  6. Worked example (do not name it): It is narrow and crowded. You hear oil hissing and someone shouting prices. It smells of garlic and something sweet. The ground is slightly wet. You are hungry before you decide anything.

Exit ticket · one room, three senses

出場券・一個房間，三種感官

Three sentences about a room in their own home. One must start with 'There is' or 'There are'.
寫自己家裡某個房間的三句話，其中一句必須用 There is 或 There are 開頭。

  1. There ______ ______ .
  2. I can hear ______ .
  3. It smells like ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Two Chinese habits collide with this week: 有 becomes 'have' instead of 'there is', and 在 becomes whichever of in/on/at the student guesses first. Both show up in the very first sentence of every place description, so fix them early.
不必備課。這週會同時撞上兩個中文習慣：「有」被翻成 have 而不是 there is，「在」則是 in／on／at 亂猜。每一篇地方描述的第一句就會出現這兩個錯，所以早點修。
