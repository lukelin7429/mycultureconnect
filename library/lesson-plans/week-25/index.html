Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 8 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • saidv.說"I'm not ready," she said.say → said · 後面接話，不接人 · 第 14 週的規則
  • askedv.問"Where were you?" he asked.問句後面用 asked，不要用 said
  • repliedv.回答"I was at the temple," she replied.回應前一句話 · 比 answered 文一點
  • whisperedv.低聲說"Don't tell anyone," he whispered.音量小 · 常帶秘密感
  • shoutedv.大喊"Watch out!" someone shouted.音量大 · 驚嘆號通常跟著來
  • explainedv.解釋"The door locks by itself," she explained.用在說明原因的句子後面
  • agreedv.同意"That's a good idea," they agreed.agree with someone · agree to a plan
  • jokedv.開玩笑說"I'll pay you next century," he joked.讓讀者知道那句不是認真的

The trap · English punctuates quotations differently

陷阱・英文的引號標點跟中文不一樣

Chinese puts the full stop outside 「」. American English puts it inside, and a comma replaces the full stop when a tag follows. Nobody teaches this, so almost every student writes it wrong for years. Show the two side by side once and it lands.
中文的句號放在「」外面。美式英文放在裡面，而且後面接 said 之類的時候，句號要換成逗號。這件事沒有人教，所以學生錯很多年。並排給他們看一次就懂了。

  1. 中文：他說：「我累了。」
  2. ✗ He said "I am tired".
  3. ✓ He said, "I am tired."　← said 後面逗號 · 句號在引號裡面
  4. ✓ "I am tired," he said.　← 前半結尾用逗號，不是句號
  5. ✓ "Are you tired?" she asked.　← 問號本來就是標點，不必再加逗號
  6. Your turn — fix this: ✗ "I don't know". He replied.

Stop using 'said' for everything

不要什麼都用 said

One Chinese word, eight English ones. Read the line aloud and pick the verb that matches how it sounds — the verb is doing half the acting.
中文一個字，英文八個。把台詞唸出來，挑一個跟語氣相符的動詞——動詞替你演掉一半的戲。

  1. "Get down from there!" she ______ .
  2. "I think the door is locked," he ______ .
  3. "Is that your bag?" the driver ______ .
  4. "Don't worry, I've done this before," she ______ .
  5. "Fine. You win," he ______ .
  6. Note: a new speaker always starts a new line.

Script It! · the boring situation

改編劇本・無聊的情境

Dom's activity needs a situation, so here is a deliberately dull one. Twelve lines, two characters, and by line four something must go wrong. Friendship or family — Dom asks that Valentine's week stay off romance.
Dom 的活動需要一個情境，這裡給一個故意很無聊的。十二行、兩個角色，第四行之前一定要出事。主題走友情或家人——Dom 指定情人節那週避開戀愛。

  1. Situation: two students are waiting for a bus that is very late.
  2. Line 1–3　Small talk. Nothing happens.
  3. Line 4　Something goes wrong.
  4. Line 5–10　They disagree about what to do.
  5. Line 11–12　One of them changes their mind.
  6. Rule: use at least four different reporting verbs, and never 'said' twice in a row.

Exit ticket · two lines, punctuated correctly

出場券・兩行對白，標點要對

One statement, one question, both with a reporting verb that is not 'said'. The punctuation is the grade.
一句陳述、一句問句，兩句的動詞都不能是 said。評分看標點。

  1. " ______ ," ______ .
  2. " ______ ?" ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Two things to fix: 說 covers said, asked, replied and told, so students use 'said' for everything — Dom's word list is the cure. And English punctuates quotations in a way Chinese does not, which is invisible until you show it side by side.
不必備課。這週修兩件事：中文一個「說」涵蓋 said、asked、replied、told，所以學生什麼都用 said——Dom 的字表就是解藥。另外英文的引號標點和中文不同，不並排對照給學生看，他們看不見。
