Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 7 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • had alreadyphr.（更早之前）已經When we arrived, the train had already left.had + 第三態 · already 夾在中間
  • had neverphr.（在那之前）從來沒有She had never seen snow before that trip.never 本身是否定，不要再加 not
  • beforeconj. / prep.在…之前He had finished his homework before dinner started.有 before 時，過去完成式常可省略——順序已經清楚了
  • afterconj. / prep.在…之後After she had left, we found her keys.同上 · 順序清楚時用過去簡單式也可以
  • by the timephr.等到…的時候By the time the police came, the thief had disappeared.這個說法幾乎一定要接過去完成式
  • realizedv.意識到She realized that she had forgotten her wallet.realize → realized · 後面很常接過去完成式
  • rememberedv.想起來He remembered that he had locked the door.remember → remembered

The trap · you only need it out of order

陷阱・不照順序才需要它

Chinese tells events in the order they happened and 了 says no more than 'done'. English can jump backwards mid-sentence, and the past perfect is the signal that it just did. Tell the story in order and past simple is enough — that is why the tense feels invisible until it is needed.
中文照事情發生的順序講，「了」只說明「完成」。英文可以在句子中途往回跳，過去完成式就是「我剛剛往回跳了」的信號。照順序講的話，過去簡單式就夠了——這就是為什麼這個時態平常看不見，需要時才出現。

  1. 照順序（不需要）：I finished my homework. Then I watched TV.　✓ 兩個都用過去簡單式
  2. 往回跳（需要）：I watched TV. I had already finished my homework.
  3. 最常見的句型：When I arrived, she had already left.　← 到的時候，她「更早之前」就走了
  4. ✗ 濫用：Yesterday I had gone to school and I had eaten lunch and I had come home.　← 照順序，全部用過去簡單式就好
  5. Your turn — choose: By the time we got there, the shop ______ (closed / had closed).

The Mystery Timeline · put it in order

神秘時間軸・排出順序

Dom's activity needs events to scramble, so here are six. Teams order them, then explain each one using 'had' — the explaining is the exercise, not the ordering.
Dom 的活動需要可以打亂的事件，這裡給六個。各組先排順序，再用 had 說明每一件——說明才是練習，排序不是。

  1. A　The red envelope was on the table at 6:00.
  2. B　Grandmother went to the temple at 6:30.
  3. C　At 7:00 the envelope was gone.
  4. D　The cat was asleep under the table all evening.
  5. E　Little brother came home at 6:45 with new candy.
  6. F　At 8:00 everyone was looking for the envelope.
  7. Now say it: By the time grandmother returned, ______ . When they started looking, little brother ______ .

Three tenses in one paragraph

一段話裡三種時態

Dom's homework asks for all three in one 180-word mystery. Build the skeleton here: background in past continuous, events in past simple, and one jump backwards in past perfect.
Dom 的作業要在一篇 180 字的懸疑故事裡用上三種。在這裡先把骨架搭好：背景用過去進行式，事件用過去簡單式，往回跳一次用過去完成式。

  1. 背景（was/were + -ing）：It ______ and everyone ______ in the kitchen.
  2. 事件（過去簡單式）：Then the lights ______ .
  3. 往回跳（had + 第三態）：Nobody remembered that someone ______ the back door.
  4. 再回到事件：When the lights came back, ______ .

Exit ticket · one jump backwards

出場券・往回跳一次

One sentence with two times in it: something that happened, and something that had happened before it.
一句話裡放兩個時間：發生的事，和在那之前就已經發生的事。

  1. When ______ , ______ had already ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed, but read the trap card first. Chinese has nothing like the past perfect, and the usual mistake after teaching it is overuse. The rule that prevents both problems: you only need it when you tell events out of order.
不必備課，但先看陷阱卡。中文沒有任何對應過去完成式的東西，而教完之後最常見的問題反而是濫用。一條規則同時解決兩邊：只有在「不照順序講」的時候才需要它。
