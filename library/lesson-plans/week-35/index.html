The trap · you do not need every word

陷阱・不必聽懂每一個字

Chinese schooling rewards catching every character; English listening punishes it. Missing a word and stopping to worry about it costs you the next ten. Native speakers miss words constantly and never notice. Say that out loud — it releases students more than any technique.
中文的學習訓練獎勵「每個字都抓到」，英文聽力卻會處罰這個習慣。漏掉一個字就停下來擔心，代價是後面十個字。母語者也一直在漏字，只是沒發現。把這句話講出來——它比任何技巧都更能解放學生。

  1. 目標不是 100%，是 70% 就繼續走。
  2. 漏掉一個字 → 不要回頭，往前聽，意思通常在下一句補回來。
  3. 先抓：誰、在哪、在做什麼、心情如何。細節之後再說。
  4. 課堂規則：這節課不准問 "What did he say?"，只能問 "What is happening?"

Watch → Pause → Predict · the points system

看→暫停→預測・計分方式

Dom's activity, with scoring that rewards the right thing. A correct guess is worth one point; a guess backed by evidence from the video is worth three — even if it turns out wrong. That scoring changes what students pay attention to.
Dom 的活動，配上獎勵正確東西的計分。猜對得一分；能說出影片裡的證據得三分——就算猜錯也一樣。這個計分方式會改變學生注意的東西。

  1. Pause 1（開頭 20 秒後）Who are these people? What is the relationship?
  2. Pause 2 What is the problem going to be?
  3. Pause 3（中段）What will he do next? Why do you think so?
  4. Pause 4（結尾前）How does this end?
  5. Scoring: 猜對 1 分 · 有證據 3 分 · 「我就是覺得」0 分
  6. Frame: I think ______ because I noticed ______ .

probably or perhaps? · how sure are you

probably 還是 perhaps？・你有多確定

Prediction needs hedging language, and Chinese hedges with 應該吧 / 可能 without grading the probability much. English grades it finely, and getting the level right is what makes a guess sound intelligent.
預測需要保留語氣，而中文的「應該吧」「可能」不太分程度。英文分得很細，用對程度，猜測才會聽起來聰明。

  1. definitely 95% → certainly 90% → probably 80% → maybe / perhaps 50% → might 40%
  2. He is definitely the father. ← 只有在證據很強時才這樣說
  3. She is probably a teacher — look at the books.
  4. Perhaps they are neighbours, but I am not sure.
  5. Your turn: 用三種不同的確定程度，各說一句關於影片的預測。

Exit ticket · one guess with evidence

出場券・一個有證據的猜測

Dom's homework asks for two predictions. Write the first here, and it must contain the word 'because'.
Dom 的作業要兩個預測，第一個在這裡寫。句子裡一定要有 because。

  1. I think ______ because ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Last week explained why listening is hard; this week gives the strategy. Students raised on 每個字都要懂 try to catch every word and freeze at the first one they miss. Teach them to keep going — meaning arrives late, and it arrives anyway.
不必備課。上週解釋了聽力為什麼難，這週給策略。從小被訓練「每個字都要懂」的學生會想抓住每一個字，漏掉一個就當機。要教他們繼續往前——意思會晚一點到，但它會到。
