Synonyms and context.

Seasonal / Taiwan connection季節與台灣連結

Vocabulary theme: fall/autumn, school, weather, sports, and Halloween leftovers are perfect synonym material.

Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 3 組同義詞 · 10 個字 · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • happyadj.快樂的She is a happy person.組 ①　最通用 · 可以形容「一個人」
  • gladadj.高興的I'm glad you came.只用在特定事件 · ✗ a glad person
  • pleasedadj.滿意的The teacher was pleased with our report.對結果滿意 · 稍正式 · pleased with
  • delightedadj.非常開心的We were delighted to hear the news.最強 · 正式場合、書信常用
  • smartadj.聰明的He is a smart student.組 ②　最通用 · 美式口語
  • cleveradj.機靈的That was a clever way to solve it.有巧思 · 形容「方法」比形容人安全（形容人有時帶狡猾意味）
  • intelligentadj.有智慧的She asked an intelligent question.正式 · 指理解力，不用來誇小聰明
  • irritatedadj.惱火的I was irritated by the noise.組 ③　最輕 · 被煩到
  • angryadj.生氣的My father was angry about the broken window.中等 · 最通用
  • furiousadj.暴怒的She was furious when she saw the mess.最強 · 不能再加 very

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Synonym Snap: teacher says a basic word; students race to give a synonym and then use it.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Synonym Snap · the call list

同義詞快閃・題目表

Dom's race. Call a basic word; the first student to answer must give a synonym AND use it in a sentence. A synonym without a sentence scores nothing — that rule is what stops this becoming a memory test.
Dom 的競賽。老師喊一個基本字，最快搶答的學生要給同義詞「並且」造句。只給字不造句不算分——這條規則才能讓它不淪為背誦測驗。

  1. big → ______
  2. happy → ______
  3. smart → ______
  4. angry → ______
  5. tired → ______
  6. scary → ______
  7. fast → ______
  8. important → ______

The trap · one Chinese word, several English ones

陷阱・中文一個字，英文好幾個

「聰明」covers smart, clever and intelligent, so students assume the three are interchangeable. They are not — English fixes each one to certain partners. Test by collocation, not by meaning.
「聰明」一個字涵蓋 smart、clever、intelligent，所以學生以為三個可以互換。不行——英文把每個字綁在固定的搭配上。要用搭配去測，不是用意思去測。

  1. ✗ He is a glad person. ✓ He is a happy person. ✓ I'm glad you came.
  2. ✗ What an intelligent trick! ✓ What a clever trick!
  3. Your turn — choose: I was very ______ (irritated / furious) by the small noise.
  4. Your turn — choose: We are ______ (glad / delighted) to invite you to our ceremony.

Which one fits?

哪一個放得進去？

Read the sentence, students pick from the week's ten words and say why. 'It sounds better' does not count; the reason must be about strength or about who or what the word is describing.
唸句子，學生從本週十個字裡挑，並說出理由。「聽起來比較好」不算；理由必須是強度，或這個字在形容誰、形容什麼。

  1. My little sister broke my model. I was ______ .
  2. Someone stole my bicycle. I was ______ .
  3. The principal was ______ with our SDG poster.
  4. She found a ______ way to carry all the books at once.
  5. We are ______ to announce the winner. (正式)

Exit ticket · same word, two feelings

出場券・同一組字，兩種感覺

This is Dom's homework in miniature: two sentences that show the difference, not two sentences that use the words.
這是 Dom 作業的縮小版：兩句話要讓差別現出來，不是只把字用進去。

  1. I was irritated when ______ .
  2. I was furious when ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Week 4 was about climbing to a stronger word. This week is the opposite lesson: synonyms are not spare parts. Each one has a place it belongs and places it does not.
不必備課。第 4 週學的是往上換一個更強的字，這週剛好相反：同義詞不是零件，不能隨便替換。每個字都有它待得住的地方，和待不住的地方。
