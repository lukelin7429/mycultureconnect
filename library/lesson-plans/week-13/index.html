The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Vocabulary Thermometer: students place words on a scale from mild to extreme and defend their choices.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Vocabulary Thermometer

單字溫度計

Dom's activity. Draw a thermometer on the board. Hand out the words shuffled; students place each one and must defend the position. Arguments about placement are the lesson, not a delay.
Dom 的活動。在白板畫一支溫度計，把字打散發下去，學生一個個貼上並說明理由。為了位置吵起來就是這堂課的重點，不是浪費時間。

  1. Set A: cool · cold · freezing · chilly
  2. Set B: like · love · adore · enjoy
  3. Set C: small · tiny · little · microscopic
  4. Set D: tired · sleepy · exhausted
  5. Rule: whoever places a word must say what is one step below it.

The trap · extreme words refuse 'very'

陷阱・極端字不收 very

Chinese happily says 非常暴怒. English does not: furious, boiling, exceptional, freezing and exhausted already contain the 'very'. They take absolutely or completely instead. This single rule makes a student sound years more advanced.
中文可以說「非常暴怒」，英文不行：furious、boiling、exceptional、freezing、exhausted 裡面已經含著 very 了。它們要接 absolutely 或 completely。光這一條規則，就能讓學生聽起來成熟好幾年。

  1. ✗ very furious ✓ absolutely furious
  2. ✗ very boiling ✓ absolutely boiling
  3. ✗ very exhausted ✓ completely exhausted
  4. ✓ very angry · very hot · very tired　← 中間那一階可以接 very
  5. Your turn — fix this: ✗ The soup was very boiling and I was very exhausted.

Sports reactions · mild to wild

看球的反應・從平淡到瘋狂

Dom's sports connection. Give the scale, then call out moments from a game; students respond at the right temperature. Shouting 'ecstatic' at a warm-up throw is the mistake to catch.
Dom 指定的運動連結。先給量尺，再喊出比賽片段，學生用對的溫度回應。熱身傳球就喊 ecstatic，正是要抓的錯。

  1. interested → keen → excited → thrilled → ecstatic
  2. The team walks onto the field. → ?
  3. Your school scores the first point. → ?
  4. Your school wins the county championship. → ?
  5. The other team scores. → annoyed / angry / furious?

Exit ticket · build your own scale

出場券・自己做一道量尺

Four words on one idea, weakest to strongest, plus one sentence using the top rung correctly. Dom's homework asks for five scales, so this is the first one done in class.
同一個概念找四個字，由弱排到強，再用最強那一階造一句正確的話。Dom 的作業要五道量尺，這是在課堂上先做掉的第一道。

  1. ______ → ______ → ______ → ______
  2. Sentence with the strongest word: ______

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed. Week 4 asked students to climb to a stronger word. This week asks something harder: know the whole ladder, and know that the top rung will not take 'very'.
不必備課。第 4 週要學生往上換一個更強的字，這週要求更難的事：認得整道梯子，並且知道最高那一階「不接 very」。
