Power vocabulary核心字彙 · 8 words · 中文與例句由 MCC 補充

  • beautifuladj.美麗的The old street looked beautiful at night.最通用的「美」
  • stunningadj.驚豔的The view from the mountain was stunning.很強 · 多半用在看得見的東西 · 極端字，不加 very
  • elegantadj.優雅的She wore an elegant dark blue dress.簡潔又講究 · 衣服、設計、解法都可以
  • dramaticadj.戲劇性的The east coast has dramatic cliffs.落差大、有衝擊力 · 風景、變化
  • ordinaryadj.普通的It was an ordinary Tuesday.中性偏平淡 · ↔ unusual
  • unusualadj.不尋常的That is an unusual name.↔ ordinary · 不帶好壞
  • impressiveadj.令人印象深刻的Winning three medals is impressive.用在成就、技術、規模
  • disappointingadj.令人失望的The ending was disappointing.-ing 是「令人…」· disappointed 才是「（人）感到…」

The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Bad Paragraph Makeover: teams transform a deliberately boring paragraph into vivid English.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Bad Paragraph Makeover · the patient

爛段落大改造・病人在此

Dom's activity needs a bad paragraph, so here is one, written badly on purpose. Hand it out, give each team six minutes, then read the versions aloud. The class votes — but a team only wins if it can say why each change is better.
Dom 的活動需要一段爛文章，這裡故意寫了一段。發下去，每組六分鐘，然後各組唸出自己的版本。全班投票——但要說得出每個更動為什麼更好，才算贏。

  1. Last week I went to a place. The food was good. There were a lot of people.
  2. The building was big and old. We took some photos. It was a good day.
  3. I was happy. The weather was nice. I want to go again.
  4. Targets: replace every 'good' · name the place · make one sentence longer than twelve words · use two words from this week.

Which word goes with which?

哪個字配哪個字？

Collocation, not meaning. All eight words mean roughly nice or not nice; only some of them fit each noun. Read the pair aloud — if it sounds odd to you, it is odd.
這是搭配問題，不是意思問題。八個字大致都在講好或不好，但只有某幾個配得上某個名詞。唸出來聽聽看——你覺得怪，它就是怪。

  1. ______ cliffs (stunning / elegant / dramatic)
  2. an ______ score in the exam (impressive / beautiful)
  3. an ______ solution to the problem (elegant / stunning)
  4. an ______ ending to the film (disappointing / ordinary)
  5. ✗ a beautiful score ✗ an elegant mountain ✗ a dramatic dress　← 為什麼怪？

-ing or -ed? · the cause or the feeling

-ing 還是 -ed？・是原因還是感受

Back in Week 2 this was exciting and excited. It returns here because it never stops causing trouble: -ing describes the thing, -ed describes the person. Saying 'I am boring' is the classic, and the class will laugh, which helps.
第 2 週的 exciting 和 excited 又回來了。它會回來是因為它從來沒有停止造成麻煩：-ing 形容那個東西，-ed 形容人。說出 I am boring 是經典錯誤，全班會笑——笑了反而記得住。

  1. The film was ______ . I was ______ . (disappointing / disappointed)
  2. ✗ I am boring. ✓ I am bored. ✓ The lesson is boring.
  3. Your turn: The news was ______ and everyone was ______ . (shocking / shocked)
  4. Your turn: I am ______ in history because history is ______ . (interested / interesting)

Exit ticket · one meal, precisely

出場券・一頓飯，精準地寫

Dom's November connection is a Thanksgiving-style descriptive paragraph without asking anyone to celebrate it. So: any meal the student remembers, three sentences, no word used twice.
Dom 指定的十一月連結是感恩節式的描寫，但不要求學生真的過這個節。所以改成：任何一頓學生記得的飯，三句話，同一個字不能用兩次。

  1. Describe a meal you remember. Three sentences.
  2. Rule: you may not use good, nice or big.
  3. Bonus: use one word from this week.

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed — the boring paragraph is written below, ready to hand out. This week is not about knowing more words; it is about the word that fits. Precision beats strength.
不必備課——那段故意寫得很無聊的文章已經備在下面，可以直接發。這週不是要學更多字，是要找到「剛好對」的那個字。精準勝過強烈。
