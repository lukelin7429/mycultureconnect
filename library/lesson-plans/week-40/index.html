The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Introduce the final movie project. Students make prediction cards and choose what they will watch for.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Movie English Guide · first half

電影英語學習單・前半

This is the guide Dom's homework asks for. Hand it out before the film. The last line matters most: choosing one thing to watch for turns passive viewing into listening practice.
這就是 Dom 作業要的學習單，看片前發下去。最後一行最重要：選定一件要盯著看的事，會把被動觀看變成聽力練習。

  1. Title: ______ 　Year: ______ 　Where it is set: ______
  2. Main character: ______ 　What they want: ______
  3. The problem in one sentence: ______
  4. Ten words I heard and want to keep: ______
  5. My job while watching (choose one): 追一個角色 · 抄下十句台詞 · 只聽語氣不看字幕 · 記下每個我聽不懂的地方
  6. (Second half — theme, opinion, review — comes after the film.)

Prediction cards · before you press play

預測卡・按下播放之前

Dom's launch activity. Watch the trailer, pause, and write the cards. Sealed until after the film, then opened — students are far more invested in a story they made a bet on.
Dom 的啟動活動。看完預告、暫停、寫卡片。封起來，看完電影再拆開——押過注的故事，學生投入程度完全不同。

  1. I predict the main character will ______ .
  2. I predict the ending will be ______ (happy / sad / surprising).
  3. One thing I think will go wrong: ______
  4. One word I expect to hear many times: ______
  5. Sign your card and seal it.

plot or theme? · the difference that trips everyone

劇情還是主題？・大家都會絆倒的分別

Students write plot summaries when asked for themes, every time. Plot is what happened; theme is what it was about. One is a list of events, the other is an idea — and only the second one can be argued about.
每次問主題，學生都會寫劇情。劇情是「發生了什麼」，主題是「關於什麼」。一個是事件清單，一個是一個想法——而且只有後者可以拿來辯論。

  1. Plot: A boy loses his dog and searches the whole city for three days.
  2. Theme: What we are willing to do for the ones we love.
  3. Test: 主題應該可以套用在另一部完全不同的電影上。劇情不行。
  4. Your turn: 想一部你看過的電影，寫一句劇情、一句主題。

Exit ticket · your one job

出場券・你的那一件任務

Each student commits to one thing they will watch for, in writing, before they leave. Public commitment is what makes it actually happen.
每個學生離開前，用寫的承諾一件他要盯著看的事。寫出來的承諾才會真的執行。

  1. While I watch, my job is to ______ .
  2. I will bring back ______ to the next class.
