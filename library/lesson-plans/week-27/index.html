The lesson, minute by minute · 課堂流程

60 minutes, four moves

  • 0–10 min
    Homework review複習作業every week · 每週相同

    Review last week's assignment. Ask 2–3 students to share. Correct one common error and celebrate one strong example.

  • 10–20 min
    Vocabulary單字every week · 每週相同

    Teach 5–10 target words. Always include: meaning, Chinese equivalent when useful, example, synonym, antonym, and/or word family.

  • 35–50 min
    Fun / application應用活動

    Story Premiere: students read a short original story and classmates award fun categories.

  • 50–57 min
    Challenge / exit ticket挑戰與出場券every week · 每週相同

    Each student produces something before leaving: a sentence, answer, mini-story, explanation, role-play, or spoken response. Keep it quick and visible.

Story planner · fill this before you write

故事規劃表・先填再寫

Ten minutes on this saves thirty minutes of staring at a blank page. Every line is one sentence. If a student cannot fill line three, they do not have a story yet — they have a situation.
花十分鐘填這張表，可以省下三十分鐘盯著空白頁。每一行寫一句。第三行填不出來的學生，手上還沒有故事，只有一個情境。

  1. Who is this about, and what do they want? ______
  2. Where and when? ______
  3. What gets in the way? ______
  4. What is the worst moment? ______
  5. What is different at the end? ______
  6. My eight power words: ______

Revision checklist · the whole unit

修稿檢查表・整個單元

Hand this out with the first draft, not before. Every line is something the class has already learned, so the checklist is a review disguised as editing.
這張表要在第一稿寫完之後才發，不要提前。每一行都是全班學過的東西，所以這張檢查表是偽裝成修稿的複習。

  1. □ 'and then' 出現幾次？超過一次就改掉。（W22）
  2. □ 每個單數可數名詞前面有沒有 a / an / the？（W23）
  3. □ 有沒有寫成 The place has…？改成 There is / There are。（W24）
  4. □ 對白的句號在引號裡面嗎？said 有沒有連用兩次？（W25）
  5. □ 有沒有一次往回跳？沒有的話，加一句 had already。（W26）
  6. □ 最後一句有沒有寫出道理？有的話刪掉。

The 60-second summary

六十秒口述

Dom asks for a spoken summary as well as the written story. Sixty seconds is about 130 words, which is four sentences — this shape fits every time.
Dom 除了書面故事還要一段口述。六十秒大約 130 字，也就是四句話——這個形狀每次都套得上。

  1. ① This is a story about ______ , who wants ______ .
  2. ② The problem is ______ .
  3. ③ The worst moment is when ______ .
  4. ④ In the end, ______ . (不要爆雷結局，停在這裡也可以)
  5. Practise it twice with a partner before the premiere.

Story Premiere · the awards

故事首映會・獎項

Dom asks classmates to award fun categories, so here are eight. Every story must win exactly one — decide that in advance and nobody sits down empty-handed.
Dom 要同學頒些有趣的獎項，這裡給八個。規定每篇故事都必須得到一個獎——事先講明，就不會有人空手坐下。

  1. Best First Line · Most Surprising Ending · Best Villain
  2. Best Use of Silence · Funniest Line of Dialogue
  3. Most Vivid Place · Character We Would Most Like to Meet
  4. Best Sentence in the Whole Class （唸出來給大家聽）
