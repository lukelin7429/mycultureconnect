The trap · no 'will' after if

陷阱・if 後面不放 will

The single most persistent conditional error among Chinese speakers, and it comes straight from 會 sitting inside the if-half. English puts the future meaning in only one half of the sentence. Chant it: if-half present, other half will.
中文母語者最頑固的條件句錯誤，來源就是「會」待在 if 那半裡。英文只在其中一半放未來的意思。當口訣唸：if 那半現在式，另一半 will。

  1. 如果明天會下雨，我就待在家。
  2. ✗ If it will rain tomorrow, I will stay home.
  3. ✓ If it rains tomorrow, I will stay home.
  4. Your turn — fix this: ✗ If you will come early, we will have more time.
  5. Your turn — fix this: ✗ I will call you if I will arrive late.

Three conditionals, three distances

三種條件句，三種距離

Not three grammar rules — three degrees of how real it is. Chinese marks none of this, so give students the question to ask: is this always true, might it happen, or is it just imagination?
這不是三條文法規則，是三種「有多真實」的程度。中文完全不標記，所以給學生一個可以問的問題：這是永遠成立、可能發生，還是純粹想像？

  1. 永遠成立（零條件）→ If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.
  2. 可能發生（第一條件）→ If I finish early, I will call you.
  3. 純粹想像（第二條件）→ If I were a bird, I would fly to Japan.
  4. 注意第二條件句往回退一格：had（不是 have）· were（不是 was）· would（不是 will）
  5. Your turn — choose: If I ______ (am / were) the principal, I would cancel Monday.

What Would You Do? · twelve scenarios

你會怎麼做？・十二個情境

Dom's activity. Read one out; students must answer in a full second conditional, not just a phrase. The silly ones loosen the class and the serious ones are where the good English appears.
Dom 的活動。唸出一個情境，學生要用完整的第二條件句回答，不能只講詞組。荒謬的題目讓氣氛鬆開，正經的題目才會長出好英文。

  1. …you found a wallet with NT$10,000 in it?
  2. …you could speak every language in the world?
  3. …you woke up as your English teacher?
  4. …you could change one rule at this school?
  5. …you had to live in another country for a year?
  6. …your phone disappeared for a month?
  7. …you could have dinner with anyone in history?
  8. …you saw your friend cheating on a test?

Exit ticket · one real, one imaginary

出場券・一句真實，一句想像

Two sentences. The first must be something that could genuinely happen this week; the second must be impossible.
兩句話。第一句要是這禮拜真的可能發生的事，第二句要是不可能的事。

  1. If ______ , I will ______ .
  2. If I ______ , I would ______ .

Prep · 備課 No preparation needed, and there is one error to hunt all lesson: will after if. Chinese says 如果明天會下雨 with 會 sitting right there in the if-half, so students carry it across. English forbids it. If-half present, other half will — say it as a chant.
不必備課，整堂課只獵一個錯：if 後面的 will。中文說「如果明天會下雨」，那個「會」就在 if 那半裡，學生會直接搬過去。英文不准。if 那半用現在式，另一半用 will——當口訣唸。
