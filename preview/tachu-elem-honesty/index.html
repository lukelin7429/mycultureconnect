2The Big Idea今天的主題

What Is Honesty?什麼是誠實？

Honesty means telling the truth — even when we make a mistake, and even when no one is watching.

誠實就是說實話——就算自己做錯了，就算沒有人在看。

5Sentences句子

You will say these with Dom. Listen, then repeat.上課時會跟 Dom 一起唸。先聽，再跟著唸。

  • It was me. I'm sorry.是我做的，對不起。
  • I made a mistake.我做錯了。
  • To be honest, …老實說……
  • That was her idea.那是她的點子。
  • Let me make it right.讓我把它處理好。

6Read and Listen聽一段短文

Tap “Play the story” and follow the words. Then try reading it yourself.按「播放整段」，眼睛跟著字走。然後自己試著唸一遍。

  1. Honesty means telling the truth, even when we make a mistake.誠實就是說實話，就算自己做錯了也一樣。
  2. If we spill something or cause a problem, we can be brave and say what happened.打翻東西或闖了禍，我們可以勇敢說出發生了什麼事。
  3. Telling the truth helps others understand and help us.說實話，別人才能了解狀況、幫助我們。
  4. Honesty also means playing fairly and following the rules.誠實也代表公平地玩、遵守規則。
  5. We do our own work and share what we really know.我們自己完成作業，寫出自己真正會的。
  6. Even when no one is watching, we can still make the right choice.就算沒有人在看，我們還是可以做對的選擇。
  7. If something goes wrong, we do not hide it. We tell the truth and help make things right.事情出了差錯，我們不隱瞞。我們說實話，並幫忙把事情處理好。
  8. Honesty builds trust. Be brave, be fair, and always choose honesty.誠實建立信任。要勇敢、要公平，永遠選擇誠實。

7Get Ready to Stand Up準備站起來

In class, Dom will read these. If it is true for you, stand up! Think about your answers now.上課時 Dom 會唸這幾句，如果說的是你，就站起來！現在先想想看。

  • Stand up if you have ever broken something by accident.曾經不小心弄壞東西的，站起來。
  • Stand up if it was hard to say “It was me.”覺得說出「是我做的」很難的，站起來。
  • Stand up if you felt better after you told the truth.說了實話之後覺得比較輕鬆的，站起來。

8Our Class Promise我們班的約定

The whole class will read this together. Practice it now so you can say it loud and clear!全班會一起大聲唸。先練習，到時候就能唸得又大聲又清楚！

  • We tell the truth, even when it is hard.就算很難，我們也說實話。
  • We play fair and follow the rules.我們公平地玩、遵守規則。
  • We do our own work.我們自己完成作業。
  • When something goes wrong, we help make it right.事情出錯時，我們幫忙把它處理好。
