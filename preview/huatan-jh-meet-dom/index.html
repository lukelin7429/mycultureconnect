1Meet Teacher Dom認識 Dom 老師

Read and listen to Dom’s story. Knowing it helps you ask better questions.邊聽邊讀 Dom 的故事。先認識她，才問得出好問題。

  1. Dom Jones comes from California, in the United States.Dom Jones 來自美國加州。
  2. She was born in Compton, California. Her mother raised thirteen children, and the family often did not have enough money.她在加州的 Compton 出生。她的媽媽養大了十三個孩子，家裡常常不夠用。
  3. She did not give up. She went to college and studied communication and women's studies.她沒有放棄。她上了大學，念傳播學和婦女研究。
  4. For almost 20 years, she has helped people stay healthy through fitness and yoga.將近二十年來，她透過健身和瑜伽幫助大家保持健康。
  5. She learned Vietnamese and Tagalog to connect with different communities in America.為了和美國不同的族群交流，她學了越南語和菲律賓的他加祿語。
  6. In 2022, she raced around the world on the TV show The Amazing Race.2022 年，她參加了電視節目《極速前進》，跑遍全世界。
  7. In 2024, a United Nations group in the USA named her a Young Leader of America.2024 年，美國的聯合國協會頒給她「美國青年領袖」的榮譽。
  8. Now she lives in Taiwan. Since December 2025, she has visited schools across Taiwan to talk about the SDGs.現在她住在台灣。從 2025 年 12 月起，她走訪台灣各地的學校，和學生談聯合國永續發展目標。
  9. Her wish is for every young person to know that their voice matters.她希望每一個年輕人都知道：你的聲音很重要。

6Keep the Conversation Going接著追問

A great interviewer listens, then asks one more question.好的採訪者會先聽，再追問一題。

  • Could you tell us more about that?可以再多說一點嗎？
  • What do you mean by ______?你說的 ______ 是什麼意思？
  • Why do you think so?你為什麼這樣想？
  • How did you feel at that moment?那時候你有什麼感覺？
  • Thank you for sharing your story.謝謝你分享你的故事。

8Interview Tips採訪小技巧

  1. Look at Dom and smile when you ask.問問題時看著 Dom，面帶微笑。
  2. Speak slowly and clearly. It is OK to be nervous!說慢一點、清楚一點。緊張也沒關係！
  3. Listen to her answer — then ask one follow-up question.認真聽她的回答，再接著問一個追問。
  4. If you don't understand, say: “Could you say that again, please?”聽不懂的時候就說：「可以請你再說一次嗎？」
  5. Take turns. Every member of the team asks at least one question.輪流問。小組每個人至少問一題。
