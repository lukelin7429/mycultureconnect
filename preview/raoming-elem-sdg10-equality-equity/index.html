2The Big Idea今天的主題

What is SDG 10?什麼是 SDG 10？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 10 is Reduced Inequalities — no one should be left behind because of where they are from, what they look like, or what they have.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 10 項是「減少不平等」——不該有人因為出身、長相或擁有的多寡而被丟下。

6Read and Listen聽一段短文

Tap “Play the story” and follow the words. Then try reading it yourself.按「播放整段」，眼睛跟著字走。然後自己試著唸一遍。

  1. Imagine a garden with many different plants.想像一座花園，裡面有很多不同的植物。
  2. If we give every plant the same cup of water, is that fair?如果每一株植物都澆一樣多的水，這樣公平嗎？
  3. A cactus needs only a little water, but a thirsty fern needs a lot.仙人掌只需要一點點水，口渴的蕨類卻需要很多。
  4. Giving everyone the same thing is called equality.給每個人一樣的東西，叫做平等。
  5. Giving each one what it needs to grow is called equity.給每一個它成長所需要的，叫做公平。
  6. People are like plants. We are all different, and we need different things.人就像植物。我們都不一樣，需要的東西也不一樣。
  7. SDG 10 asks us to make sure no one is left behind.SDG 10 要我們確保沒有人被丟下。
  8. When we notice what others need and help them, everyone can grow together.當我們留意別人需要什麼、並且幫助他們，每個人都能一起成長。
