What Is Gratitude?什麼是感恩？

Gratitude means noticing what other people do for you — and then saying thank you, or giving something back.

感恩，就是注意到別人為你做了什麼——然後說聲謝謝，或是回饋一點什麼。

3 Things You Can Do Before You Graduate畢業前可以做的 3 件事

1
Say thank you to a helper — and use their name.向一位幫助你的人說謝謝——叫出他的名字。

Give your card to the person you wrote it for.把卡片親手交給你寫給的那個人。

Help a younger student. One day, YOU will be the one who gives back.幫學弟妹一個忙。有一天，你也會是回饋母校的那個人。
