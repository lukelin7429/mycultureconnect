What Is SDG 17?什麼是 SDG 17？

In 2015, every UN member country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 17 is the glue that holds the other 16 together: governments, companies, schools and people working as partners and sharing money, technology and knowledge.

2015 年，聯合國所有會員國訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 17 項是把其他 16 項黏在一起的膠水：政府、企業、學校和每個人成為夥伴，分享資金、科技和知識。
