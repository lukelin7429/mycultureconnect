What Is Honesty?什麼是誠實？

Honesty means telling the truth — even when we make a mistake, and even when no one is watching.

誠實就是說實話——就算自己做錯了，就算沒有人在看。

truth實話mistake錯誤fair公平trust信任

🙋 Stand Up If…如果是你，就站起來！

  1. Stand up if you have ever broken something by accident.曾經不小心弄壞東西的，站起來。
  2. Stand up if it was hard to say “It was me.”覺得說出「是我做的」很難的，站起來。
  3. Stand up if you felt better after you told the truth.說了實話之後覺得比較輕鬆的，站起來。
Telling the truth takes courage. That is why it matters.說實話需要勇氣。所以它才重要。

3 Honest Things to Do This Week這週可以做的 3 件誠實的事

1
When you make a mistake, say “It was me” right away.做錯事的時候，馬上說「是我做的」。
2
Do your own work — even when it is hard.自己完成作業——就算很難。
3
Give credit: “That was ______'s idea.”把功勞歸給對的人：「那是 ______ 的點子。」

✉️ Our Class Promise我們班的約定

  1. We tell the truth, even when it is hard.就算很難，我們也說實話。
  2. We play fair and follow the rules.我們公平地玩、遵守規則。
  3. We do our own work.我們自己完成作業。
  4. When something goes wrong, we help make it right.事情出錯時，我們幫忙把它處理好。
Read it out loud together — then seal it with a high five!一起大聲唸出來——然後擊掌為約！
