What Is SDG 5?什麼是 SDG 5？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 5 is Gender Equality: girls and boys should have the same chances and the same respect.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 5 項是「性別平等」：女生和男生都應該有一樣的機會、一樣的尊重。

What Is a Stereotype?什麼是刻板印象？

A stereotype puts people in a box: “Girls can't play soccer.” “Boys don't cry.” But a real person is much bigger than any box.

刻板印象，就是把人裝進框框：「女生不會踢足球。」「男生不能哭。」但是一個真實的人，比任何框框都大得多。
