What Is Bullying?什麼是霸凌？

Bullying is hurting someone on purpose, usually again and again, when they cannot easily make it stop. It can be hitting, mean words, leaving someone out, or messages online.

霸凌是故意傷害別人，而且通常一再發生，對方很難讓它停下來。它可以是動手、說難聽的話、排擠別人，或是網路上的訊息。

Bystander or Upstander?旁觀者，還是挺身而出的人？

A bystander sees bullying and does nothing. An upstander does something: stand next to the person, say “Stop, that's not OK,” or tell a teacher. You don't have to be brave alone.

旁觀者看到霸凌，什麼都不做。挺身而出的人會採取行動：站到對方身邊、說「停，這樣不對」，或去告訴老師。你不需要一個人勇敢。

✉️ Our Class Promise我們班的約定

  1. We notice when someone is left out.有人被冷落，我們會注意到。
  2. We speak up kindly.我們溫和地說出來。
  3. We invite — we do not exclude.我們邀請，不排擠。
  4. We tell an adult when someone is hurt.有人受傷，我們會告訴大人。
Read it out loud together — then seal it with a high five!一起大聲唸出來——然後擊掌為約！
