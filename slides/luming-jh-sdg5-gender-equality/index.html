What Is SDG 5?什麼是 SDG 5？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 5 is Gender Equality: girls and boys deserve the same rights, the same chances and the same respect.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 5 項是「性別平等」：女生和男生都應該有一樣的權利、一樣的機會、一樣的尊重。

equal平等rights權利chance機會respect尊重

What Is a Stereotype?什麼是刻板印象？

A stereotype puts a whole group of people in one box: “Girls are bad at math.” “Boys don't cry.” But a box is too small for a real person.

刻板印象，就是把一整群人裝進同一個框框：「女生數學不好。」「男生不能哭。」但是一個框框，裝不下一個真實的人。

Taiwan Leads the Way台灣走在前面

Taiwan elected its first woman president in 2016. Today women hold about 42% of the seats in our legislature — one of the highest shares in Asia.

台灣在 2016 年選出第一位女總統。現在立法院大約有 42% 的席次是女性——是亞洲最高的國家之一。
