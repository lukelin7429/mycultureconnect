What is SDG 10?什麼是 SDG 10？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 10 is Reduced Inequalities — no one should be left behind because of where they are from, what they look like, or what they have.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 10 項是「減少不平等」——不該有人因為出身、長相或擁有的多寡而被丟下。

🎮 Game Time! 遊戲時間 Q1
Two students need to see over a wall. One is tall, one is short. What is FAIR?兩個學生要看過一道牆，一高一矮。怎樣才叫「公平」？
✅
Equality gives everyone the same. Equity gives everyone what they need to reach the same view.平等是「給一樣的」；公平是「給他所需要的」，讓大家都看得到一樣的風景。

✋ Which One Helps Most?哪一個最有效？

Vote with your hands — 1, 2, 3, or 4 fingers.用手指投票——比 1、2、3 或 4。
1
Learn and use people's names記住並叫出別人的名字
2
Invite someone into your group邀請別人加入你的小組
