The River Runs Past Your School那條溪，就從你們學校旁邊流過

The Dongluo River — the old Zhuoshui River — runs along the edge of Tianwei, through Pitou and Erlin, and reaches the Taiwan Strait at Fusing. A plastic wrapper dropped into a drain here can be in the sea in a few days.

東螺溪（舊濁水溪）沿著田尾鄉界流過，經過埤頭、二林，最後在福興鄉出海。在這裡掉進水溝的一個塑膠包裝，幾天後就可能在海裡。

12 Oxen Left只剩下 12 頭牛

For sixty years, Fangyuan farmers have walked oxen onto the mudflat to harvest oysters. Machines took over. About twelve working oxen remain — the last place on Earth.

六十年來，芳苑蚵農趕著黃牛走進潮間帶採蚵。機械取代之後，只剩約十二頭海牛——地球上最後一個。

✋ Which Helps the Ocean Most?哪一件事對海洋幫助最大？

Rank these four from most helpful to least. Argue for your answer!把這四件事從幫助最大排到最小。說出你的理由！
1
Refusing a plastic straw once拒絕一支塑膠吸管
2
Picking up litter near your school drain撿起學校水溝旁的垃圾
3
Getting your whole family to use one less plastic bag a day讓全家每天少用一個塑膠袋
4
Telling ten people what you learned today把今天學到的告訴十個人
There is no single right answer — but the last two multiply. One person's habit becomes many.沒有標準答案——但後面兩個會「放大」。一個人的習慣會變成很多人的習慣。
