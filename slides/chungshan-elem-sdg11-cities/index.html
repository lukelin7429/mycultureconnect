Once, Lukang Was Taiwan's No. 2 City鹿港，曾經是全台第二大城

From 1784, ships sailed straight from Fujian to Lukang's harbor. It grew so big that people said: first Tainan, second Lukang, third Monga.

1784 年起，船隻從福建直航鹿港。鹿港繁榮到大家都說：「一府、二鹿、三艋舺」。
