What Is a Global Mindset?什麼是國際觀？

A global mindset means being curious about the world, respecting people who are different, and being brave enough to connect. You do not need a passport to start. You can start right here in Tianzhong.

國際觀就是對世界好奇、尊重和自己不一樣的人，而且有勇氣去連結。你不需要護照才能開始，在田中這裡就可以開始。

Tianzhong Is Already Global田中早就很國際

In November, about 17,000 runners came to the Tianzhong Marathon. Fifty runners named Tanaka flew in from Japan, because 田中 is “Tanaka” in Japanese! And who won the cheering contest? Class 807!
