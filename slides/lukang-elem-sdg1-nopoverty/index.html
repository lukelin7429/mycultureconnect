What Is SDG 1?什麼是 SDG 1？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 1 is No Poverty: no one should live without food, clean water, a safe home, school or a doctor because they do not have enough money.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 1 項是「消除貧窮」：不該有人因為沒有錢，就沒有食物、乾淨的水、安全的家、學校或醫生。

Lukang Has Been Rich — and Poor鹿港富過，也窮過

About 200 years ago, Lukang was one of Taiwan's busiest ports. Then sand filled the harbor, the big ships stopped coming, and many families lost their work. Today crafts, food and visitors bring the town to life again.

大約兩百年前，鹿港是台灣最熱鬧的港口之一。後來港口被泥沙淤積，大船不再來，很多家庭失去了工作。現在，工藝、美食和遊客又讓小鎮熱鬧起來。
