My Hometown: Surf City USA我的家鄉：衝浪之城

Huntington Beach is a beach city in Southern California. Its nickname is “Surf City USA.” It has a long pier that walks you out over the ocean, and every summer, surfers from around the world come to compete there.

杭廷頓海灘是南加州的海邊城市，綽號叫「美國衝浪之城」。那裡有一座長長的碼頭，可以一路走到海面上；每年夏天，世界各地的衝浪好手都會來這裡比賽。

What Is Thanksgiving?什麼是感恩節？

Families come together for a big dinner: turkey, mashed potatoes, and pumpkin pie. Before eating, many families go around the table, and each person says one thing they are thankful for.

全家人聚在一起吃大餐：火雞、馬鈴薯泥和南瓜派。開動之前，很多家庭會繞著餐桌輪流，每個人說一件自己感謝的事。

Halloween: The American Way萬聖節：美國人怎麼過

At Chingshan, you dressed up as ghosts, witches and pirates. In America, kids in costumes walk from house to house in their neighborhood. They knock on the door and say, “Trick or treat!” Neighbors give them candy.

在青山，你們扮成鬼、女巫和海盜。在美國，穿著變裝的小孩會在社區裡一家一家地走，敲門說「Trick or treat！」，鄰居就會給他們糖果。

How Do People Get Around?美國人怎麼移動？

In Taiwan, scooters are everywhere. In most of America, families drive cars, because towns are far apart. In California, you can get a driver's license at 16.

在台灣，到處都是機車。在美國大部分地方，家家戶戶開車，因為城鎮之間離得很遠。在加州，16 歲就可以考駕照。

🎮 Game Time! 遊戲時間 Q4
On a hot day in Yuanlin, it is 30°C. Americans use Fahrenheit. What is 30°C in °F?員林大熱天是攝氏 30 度。美國人用華氏，30°C 是華氏幾度？
✅
86°F! Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. So if an American says “It's 100 degrees!”, that's about 38°C.華氏 86 度！乘以 9、除以 5、再加 32。所以美國人說「100 度！」的時候，其實大約是攝氏 38 度。

Many Cultures, One Neighborhood很多文化，同一個社區

Near my hometown is Little Saigon, one of the biggest Vietnamese communities outside Vietnam. My neighbors speak Spanish, Vietnamese, Tagalog, Korean and Chinese. I can speak Vietnamese and Tagalog, too!

我家鄉附近有「小西貢」，是越南以外最大的越南社區之一。我的鄰居說西班牙語、越南語、他加祿語、韓語和中文。我也會說越南語和他加祿語喔！
