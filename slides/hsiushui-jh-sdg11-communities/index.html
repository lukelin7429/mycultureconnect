What Is SDG 11?什麼是 SDG 11？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 11 asks us to make cities and communities safe, inclusive, resilient and sustainable: places where everyone can live well.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 11 項要讓城市與社區安全、包容、有韌性、可永續——讓每個人都能好好生活。

Three Ways to Build a Strong Community讓社區強大的三個方法

1
Play together: sports keep a community healthy and connected.一起運動：運動讓社區健康、彼此連結。
2
Keep our heritage: old music and old places tell us who we are.守護文化：老音樂和老地方告訴我們自己是誰。
3
Welcome everyone: a community is stronger when every voice belongs.歡迎每個人：每個聲音都被接納，社區就更強大。

Music Older Than Your Great-Grandparents比曾祖父母還老的音樂

Nanguan and Beiguan came to Taiwan with settlers from Fujian. In nearby Lukang, Nanguan clubs have played for about 200 years. Your Nanbeiguan ensemble took the stage at the National Student Nanbeiguan Music Competition in both 2024–25 and 2025–26.

南管和北管隨著福建移民來到台灣。在隔壁的鹿港，南管館閣已經演奏了大約兩百年。你們的南北管樂團，113、114 學年度連續站上全國學生南北管音樂比賽的舞台。
