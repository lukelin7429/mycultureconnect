What is SDG 2?什麼是 SDG 2？

In 2015, every country in the world agreed on 17 Global Goals to reach by 2030. Goal number 2 is Zero Hunger — no one on Earth should go hungry.

2015 年，全世界所有國家一起訂下 17 項全球目標，希望在 2030 年前達成。第 2 項就是「消除飢餓」——地球上不該有人挨餓。

✋ Which One Helps Most?哪一個最有效？

Vote with your hands — 1, 2, 3, or 4 fingers.用手指投票——比 1、2、3 或 4。
1
Finish your school lunch把營養午餐吃完
2
Buy the 'ugly' vegetables買賣相不好的蔬菜
3
Share extra food with a classmate把多的食物分給同學
4
Tell your family what you learned today回家告訴家人今天學到什麼
There is no wrong answer — every one of these works. The biggest one is #4, because it multiplies.沒有錯的答案，四個都有效。最強的是第 4 個，因為它會擴散出去。
