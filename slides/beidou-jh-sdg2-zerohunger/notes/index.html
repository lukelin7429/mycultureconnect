Pacing — you have 45 minutes The core lesson is about 46 minutes and the bonus slides add another 9. That is deliberately more than one period: if you finish the core early, go to the BONUS slides rather than filling time. The slowest slides on purpose are the True/False round and the two games — stretch those.
How the slides work Quiz slides: take a show-of-hands vote first, then tap any option and the correct answer lights up with an explanation. True/False: tap each row separately. The animation on slide 3 plays when you click the thumbnail. Nothing is timed — you control every reveal.
1 1 min1 min A Bilingual Lesson for Junior High Students

Open big. Point at the SDG 2 badge. 'Today we are talking about food — and about people who don't have any.' Read the three numbers out loud, slowly.
2 2 min3 min Hello, I'm Dom!

Introduce yourself. Compton → Taiwan. Keep it personal, not a CV. End with the raise-your-hand question and WAIT for hands — don't rush past it.
3 2.5 min6 min Be the Change

Click the thumbnail to play. Before it starts: 'Listen for one English word you already know.' After it ends, take 3–4 answers. This is your first energy check.
4 3 min8 min Stand Up If…

Do all three in a row, don't explain between them. By #3 only a few stay standing — let the silence sit for 2 seconds, then deliver the punch line. This is the emotional anchor of the whole lesson.
5 2 min10 min What is SDG 2?

Keep it short — this is the only 'lecture' slide. Say the 4 keywords out loud and have them repeat each one.
6 3 min14 min Q1: How many people in the world don't have enough food?

Read the question. Take a vote by show of hands for A/B/C/D BEFORE revealing. Then tap any option to reveal. React to how many got it right.
7 2 min16 min of all the food in the world is never eaten

Let the big number land. Ask the question at the bottom and take 2–3 answers. Do NOT answer it yourself — the next slides answer it.
8 3 min18 min Q2: Food waste causes what share of the world's greenhouse gases?

Same routine: vote first, then tap to reveal. The '3rd biggest polluter' line usually gets a reaction — use it.
9 5 min24 min True or False? 快問快答

Four statements. For each: hands up ⭕ or ❌, then tap that row to reveal. Slowest slide on purpose — good place to slow down if you're ahead of time.
10 3 min26 min Q3: Where is the most food wasted?

Vote, then reveal. The point is 'we can fix it ourselves' — say that line twice.
11 4 min30 min 30-Second Challenge

Energy slide. Actually count out loud. Let them shout. The hints are on screen if they stall. Celebrate whatever number they get.
12 4 min34 min Turn and Talk

60 seconds of pair talk. Walk around while they talk. Then call on 3 pairs. Make them use the sentence frame on screen.
13 3 min38 min Which One Helps Most?

Hands-up vote 1/2/3/4. Then tap Reveal. Emphasise that #4 multiplies — 'you tell one person, they tell one person.'
14 2 min40 min 3 Things You Can Do This Week

Three actions, read each one, have the class repeat #1 together.
15 3 min42 min Say It With Me

Choral repetition. Say each line, they repeat. Go faster each round — make it a game by the 3rd pass.
16 2.5 min— Q4: Which of these is a fruit, not a vegetable?BONUS · skip if short

BONUS — skip if short on time. Light relief, the tomato answer usually surprises them.
17 2.5 min— Q5: How long can most food last AFTER the 'best before' date?BONUS · skip if short

BONUS — skip if short on time. Practical and slightly surprising; good for older classes.
18 4 min— Bonus Game: Save It or Waste It?BONUS · skip if short

BONUS GAME — your main time-filler. Thumbs up/down on each item. You can invent more items beyond the list if you still have time.
19 2 min44 min Tell Dom What You Learned

Point at the QR. Give them 2 minutes to post. Read one or two posts out loud if any come in.
20 1 min46 min Be kind, be brave, be the change.

Close strong. Say the line, then have the whole class say it back to you. Thank them by name of the school.
