The Earth Wears a Blanket地球蓋著一條被子

Gases in the air hold the sun's heat like a blanket — without it we would freeze. But burning oil, gas and coal makes the blanket thicker, and the Earth gets too warm.

空氣裡的氣體像被子一樣留住太陽的熱——沒有它我們會凍僵。但燒石油、天然氣和煤，讓被子越來越厚，地球就太熱了。

warmer: Taiwan today, compared with 110 years ago

台灣現在比 110 年前熱了 1.6°C

Xizhou Grows Trees for Taiwan溪州，為台灣種樹

Taiwan's largest landscape-tree nursery park is right here in Xizhou. Trees take in carbon dioxide, give shade and cool the air. Your township is already helping the whole island.
