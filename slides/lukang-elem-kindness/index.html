What Is Kindness?什麼是仁慈？

Kindness means you see what someone needs — and you do something nice to help.

仁慈，就是看見別人需要什麼——然後做一件好事幫助他。

Kindness in a Narrow Lane窄巷裡的仁慈

Lukang has an old lane less than one meter wide. When two people meet, one must step aside. That is why people call it Gentleman's Lane.

鹿港有一條不到一公尺寬的老巷子。兩個人相遇時，一定要有一個人側身讓路。所以大家叫它「君子巷」。

lane巷子narrow窄step aside讓路polite有禮貌

🎮 Game Time! 遊戲時間 Q3
In Gentleman's Lane, you meet a grandma carrying a heavy basket. What is the kind thing to do?在君子巷裡，你遇到一位提著重籃子的阿嬤。怎麼做才是仁慈？
✅
Step aside and smile. Letting someone go first is a small kindness anyone can do.側身讓路，再加一個微笑。讓別人先走，是每個人都做得到的小小仁慈。
