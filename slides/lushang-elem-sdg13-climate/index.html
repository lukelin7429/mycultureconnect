The Earth Wears a Blanket地球蓋著一條被子

Gases in the air hold the sun's heat like a blanket — without it we would freeze. But burning oil, gas and coal makes the blanket thicker, and the Earth gets too warm.

空氣裡的氣體像被子一樣留住太陽的熱——沒有它我們會凍僵。但燒石油、天然氣和煤，讓被子越來越厚，地球就太熱了。

1.6°C

warmer: Taiwan today, compared with 110 years ago

台灣現在比 110 年前熱了 1.6°C

It sounds small. But think of your body: 37°C is fine — 38.6°C is a fever.聽起來很少。但想想你的身體：37°C 很正常，38.6°C 就是發燒了。

Fangyuan's Forest in the Sea芳苑的海上森林

On Fangyuan's coast grows a mangrove forest — trees that live in salt water. People planted the first ones in the 1980s. Now crabs, birds and fish live there, and the trees take in carbon dioxide and protect the coast from big waves.

芳苑的海岸長著一片紅樹林——住在鹹水裡的樹。1980 年代有人種下第一批，現在螃蟹、鳥和魚都住在那裡；這些樹會吸收二氧化碳，還保護海岸不被大浪沖壞。
