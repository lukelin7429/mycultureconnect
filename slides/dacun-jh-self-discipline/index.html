What Is Self-Discipline?什麼是自律？

Self-discipline is doing what you need to do, even when you don't feel like it. It is a promise you make to yourself, and keep.

自律，就是就算不想做，還是去做該做的事。它是你對自己許下的承諾，而且說到做到。

The Marshmallow Test棉花糖實驗

At Stanford University, researchers gave young children a choice: eat one marshmallow now, or wait alone for about 15 minutes and get two.

美國史丹佛大學的研究人員讓小朋友選：現在吃一顆棉花糖，或是自己一個人等大約 15 分鐘，就能吃兩顆。
