What Is Gratitude?什麼是感恩？

Gratitude means noticing what other people do for you — and then saying thank you, or giving something back.

感恩，就是注意到別人為你做了什麼——然後說聲謝謝，或是回饋一點什麼。

Why Does Changhua Still Thank Dr. Lai He?為什麼彰化人到現在還在感謝賴和？

Lai He studied at your school and became a doctor in Changhua. Poor patients who could not pay wrote him a note: “I will pay you later.”

賴和是你們學校的校友，後來在彰化當醫生。付不出錢的窮人，會寫一張借據給他：「我以後再還。」

At the end of every year, what did Dr. Lai do with those notes?每到年底，賴和醫師怎麼處理那些借據？
✅
He burned them, so no poor family had to start the new year in debt. People called him “the Matsu of Changhua.”他把借據全部燒掉，讓窮人不用「欠錢過年」。大家叫他「彰化媽祖」。
