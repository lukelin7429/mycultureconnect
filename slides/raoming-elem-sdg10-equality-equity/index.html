What is SDG 10?什麼是 SDG 10？

In 2015, every country agreed on 17 Global Goals for 2030. Goal 10 is Reduced Inequalities — no one should be left behind because of where they are from, what they look like, or what they have.

2015 年，全世界所有國家訂下 17 項全球目標，要在 2030 年前達成。第 10 項是「減少不平等」——不該有人因為出身、長相或擁有的多寡而被丟下。

Two Big Words兩個重要的字

Equality means everyone gets the same thing. Equity means everyone gets what they need, so everyone can reach the same goal.

平等（equality），是每個人拿到一樣的東西。公平（equity），是每個人拿到自己需要的東西，讓大家都能到達同一個目標。

Same Rights, Fair Help一樣的權利，公平的幫助

Your school has taught you about children's rights: every child has the right to learn, to play and to be safe. Some children need a little extra help to enjoy those rights. That is not special treatment — that is fair.

你們學校教過兒童權利公約：每個孩子都有學習、遊戲、受保護的權利。有些孩子需要多一點幫助，才能真正享有這些權利。這不是特別待遇，這就是公平。
