Oxen in the Sea!走進海裡的牛！

In Fangyuan, next to Erlin, farmers walk oxen onto the mudflat to pick oysters. Only about a dozen of these oxen are left in the world.

在二林隔壁的芳苑，蚵農牽著牛走進潮間帶採蚵仔。這樣的「海牛」，全世界只剩大約十幾頭。
