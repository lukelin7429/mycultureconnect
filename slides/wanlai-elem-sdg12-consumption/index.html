🕰 From Garbage Island to Recycling Champion從垃圾之島到回收模範生

  1. 1993
    Only 70% of trash is collected. The rest is dumped or burned.只有七成垃圾被收走，其餘被亂丟或燒掉
  2. 1996
    Trash must not touch the ground — you bring it to the truck垃圾不落地——自己把垃圾拿去垃圾車
  3. 1997
    The four-in-one recycling program begins推動「四合一資源回收計畫」
  4. 1998
    Each person throws away 1.14 kg a day每人每天丟 1.14 公斤垃圾
  5. Today
    Under 0.4 kg a day — and over half is recycled每天不到 0.4 公斤，超過一半被回收

Where Does Your Phone Go?你的手機最後去了哪裡？

Metals dug from a mine. A factory far away. A ship, a shop, your hand — and a few years later, a drawer. Old phones still hold gold and copper that could be used again.

從礦場挖出的金屬、遙遠的工廠，再經過船、商店，到你手上——幾年後，進了抽屜。舊手機裡還有可以再利用的金和銅。
