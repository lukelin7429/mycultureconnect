What Is SDG 16?SDG 16 是什麼？

Peace: people are safe. Justice: the rules are fair for everyone. Strong institutions: schools, courts and town halls that keep it that way — and listen to people.

和平：大家都安全。正義：規則對每個人都公平。健全制度：學校、法院、公所這些地方，讓這一切維持下去，也願意聽人民的聲音。

A School Built on a Gift一份禮物蓋起來的學校

Wanlai is named after Mr. Yang Wanlai. He lived in Japan, but he gave land and money so children here could have a school. A school is an institution: it belongs to everyone, and it must treat everyone fairly.

萬來國小的校名，是紀念旅日華僑楊萬來先生。他捐地又捐錢，讓這裡的孩子有學校可以讀。學校就是一種「制度」：它屬於每一個人，也必須公平對待每一個人。

Be an Upstander當一個挺身而出的人

A bystander sees bullying and does nothing. An upstander does something: stand next to the person, say “Stop, that's not OK,” or tell a teacher. You don't have to be brave alone.

旁觀者看到霸凌，什麼都不做。挺身而出的人會採取行動：站到對方身邊、說「停，這樣不對」，或去告訴老師。你不需要一個人勇敢。

✉️ Our Class Promise我們班的約定

  1. In our class, everyone gets a turn.在我們班，每個人都有機會。
  2. We solve problems by talking, not fighting.我們用說的解決問題，不用打的。
  3. No one is left out.不讓任何人落單。
  4. Every voice counts — even the quiet ones.每個聲音都算數——安靜的聲音也是。
Three classes, one promise — read it out loud together!三個班，一個約定——一起大聲唸出來！
