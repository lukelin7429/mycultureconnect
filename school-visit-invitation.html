邀請學校 · Invitation for Schools

Dom Jones 校園之旅

讓學生用真實英語，跟世界對話。

Dom Jones School Tour — real English conversations that connect students with the world.

核心三承諾 · What Dom Brings

每一場訪校，都圍繞這三件事

1

英語教育

學生直接與美國籍主持人 Dom Jones 全英語互動問答，不是看影片、是活生生開口說。

Students speak directly with Dom Jones, a native English-speaking American host — not a video, but a live conversation.

2

全球連結

Dom 把彰化教室和洛杉磯、聯合國協會連起來，讓孩子知道自己的聲音能被世界聽見。

Dom connects Changhua classrooms with Los Angeles and the United Nations Association, showing students their voice can reach the world.

3

品格教育

每場都以同理心貫穿，帶入品格教育的核心價值，讓孩子把善意化為實際行動。

Every visit is grounded in empathy, weaving character education into values students can put into action right away.

看得見的實績 · Proven Track Record

已經走過 19+ 場，非常成熟

19 所學校

已完成校長專訪影片（Voices in Education 教育之聲），每位校長親口談為什麼投入教育、英語與國際教育對孩子的意義。

19 principal interviews completed — each principal sharing why they chose education, and what English and global learning mean for their students.

25+ 支影片

校園新聞影片，每場訪校都留下紀錄，學生自己也能上鏡。

25+ campus news videos document every visit, often featuring the students themselves.

怎麼安排 · How It Works

選一個星期二，我們就到貴校

有興趣的話，我們可以利用某個週二的上午或下午到貴校訪問，讓校方挑選互動主題（SDGs 或品格教育），可以是一個班級，也可以對更多學生，由校方安排即可。選定日期後，我們會再進一步討論行程與活動細節。

If you're interested, we can visit your school on a Tuesday morning or afternoon. Your school selects the interactive theme (SDGs or Character Education) — for a single class or a larger group, whichever works best for you. Once a date is set, we'll follow up to confirm the full schedule and details.
